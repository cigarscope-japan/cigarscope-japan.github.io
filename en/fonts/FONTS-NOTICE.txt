Status of this file (record corrected 2026-11-08 after independent
audit — the distinctions below replace any earlier loose wording):
  * The WOFF2 above is stored and served byte-for-byte as received
    from Google Fonts; it has not been modified since acquisition.
  * In relation to the upstream original font, however, it is NOT
    byte-identical: it is a Google-generated distribution that has
    undergone format conversion (WOFF2) and Latin subsetting. It is
    therefore handled as a Modified Version under OFL 1.1, with the
    licence text and copyright notice retained alongside it in this
    directory (OFL-PublicSans.txt and this notice).

    Libre Franklin) is under OFL 1.1 (Copyright 2015 The Public Sans
    Project Authors); the U.S. GSA's modifications are released as
    CC0. The combined work as distributed is governed by OFL 1.1; the
    CC0 layer does not replace the OFL for the whole.

    compared with the official uswds/public-sans LICENSE.md. The
    operative sections (DEFINITIONS through TERMINATION) are
    identical (whitespace-normalised), and the phrase "with Reserved
