Puro Som Debuts Sine Tempore, a Maryland Boutique's First Cigar

Puro Som, a new boutique based in Columbia, Maryland, has formally debuted its first cigar, "Sine Tempore." It is a box-pressed toro, 6 x 54, with a Cameroon wrapper, an Estelí binder and Estelí, Jalapa and Condega filler, the leaf aged at least three years. It is medium-plus, made at an undisclosed boutique factory in Estelí, at $14 per cigar in 20-count boxes. Founder Joshua W. Kafer is a cigar sommelier. The cigar first appeared on June 13, 2026, at Broadleaf Tobacco in Severna Park, Maryland, and is now on sale at three stores in the state; production figures were not disclosed.
