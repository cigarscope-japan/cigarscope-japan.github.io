Limited Edition · United States

Espinosa's Las 6 Provincias MIA Arrives at Stores — Final Chapter Honors Miami, Limited to 20,000 Cigars

The seventh and final release in Espinosa Premium Cigars' limited "Las 6 Provincias" line, "MIA," has begun arriving at stores. The series is named for the six provinces of Cuba at independence; this final chapter is an homage to Miami, the unofficial seventh province. It is a Nicaraguan puro made at the San Lotano factory, in a 6 1/4 x 55 toro extra, at $18 per cigar and $360 per 20-count box. Production is limited to 1,000 boxes of 20 (20,000 cigars). It was unveiled at the PCA trade show in April 2026 and released on November 19, 2026.

Espinosa's La Zona Palooza Fan Festival Marks 10 Years with Over 300 Guests

Espinosa Premium Cigars' fan festival La Zona Palooza marked its tenth year, drawing more than 300 guests over three days. The event-exclusive 601 La Bomba Warhead Bombazo was on sale, chef Guy Fieri appeared on the second day for a two-hour session with fans, and the final night closed with a gala at the botanical garden in Hialeah Gardens.

Limited Edition · Nicaragua

Espinosa's 601 La Bomba Warhead TAA 2026 Ships in November

Espinosa Premium Cigars will ship a Tobacconists' Association of America (TAA) exclusive version of its annual La Bomba Warhead series in November. The 6 x 55 box-pressed toro uses a Mexican San Andrés wrapper over Nicaraguan binder and fillers, made at the San Lotano factory in Ocotal. Production is limited to 1,250 boxes of ten (12,500 cigars) at $16 per cigar, $160 per box.
