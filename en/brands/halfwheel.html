Halfwheel Editors Reveal the Gear They Still Use — a Switch to Soft Flame and a $600 Ashtray

Three Halfwheel editors have revealed the gear they actually keep using, in the second year of the annual feature — the premise being that what you keep using is the real thing. Brooks Whittington lists the S.T. Dupont slim cigar cutter (fast replacing his beloved XIKAR XO), the Palió Veneto (a four-flame lighter, now his everyday lighter for large ring gauges, with a cigar rest), a Boveda 50-count humidor (one 320-gram pack holding humidity for about a year) and the Czech hand-blown glass Czevitrum Salamander ashtray (the only carryover from last year). Charlie Minato lists the Fox 749 cutter (used for every cigar he reviewed this year), the IM Corona 69 Double Corona (his only big change this year — a switch to soft flame; a two-flame Japanese soft-flame lighter equivalent to the S.T. Dupont Ligne 2, bought about 15 years ago and still in service), the Blazer PB207 (for touch-ups and relights), the Sonic cabinet humidifier (three cabinets holding over 5,000 cigars) and the OH!VAL ashtray (bought in 2023 for $222, now $600 — "for $600 I'd buy something cheaper," he says). Patrick Lagreid lists the S.T. Dupont table lighter (two flames, large tank), the XIKAR MTX cigar scissors (his only purchase of the past year, with a lighter purge, flame adjustment and bottle opener), the Stinky original ashtray (15–20 years in use) and plastic bins with Boveda for storage (two cooler doors plus traditional humidors). All three articles were published November 27, 2026, with photos of the actual products.
