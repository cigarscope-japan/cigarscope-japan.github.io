New Release · Dominican Republic

Kristoff Unveils the AI-Designed Kristoff.ai, Bound for a Full Launch at PCA 2027

Kristoff Cigars has announced Kristoff.ai, a cigar whose blend was designed using artificial intelligence. AI analysis of thousands of reviews, top lists and podcast discussions was used to extract the patterns enthusiasts consistently praise. The 6 1/4 x 52 cigar pairs an Ecuadorian Sumatra wrapper with all-Nicaraguan binder and filler, and is in a limited soft launch of 500 ten-count bundles at $114.99; the full launch is planned for the 2027 PCA trade show in 20-count boxes.
