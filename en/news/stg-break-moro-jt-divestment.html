STG Completes Sale of BREAK and Moro Fine-Cut Brands to JT for DKK 1.3 Billion

Scandinavian Tobacco Group completed the divestment of its BREAK and Moro fine-cut tobacco brands to Japan Tobacco on October 1, 2026, for DKK 1.3 billion, and raised its 2026 free-cash-flow guidance on the back of the deal.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
October 1, 2026
Maker
Scandinavian Tobacco Group

Scandinavian Tobacco Group (STG) has completed the divestment of the fine-cut tobacco brands BREAK and Moro to Japan Tobacco (JT), effective October 1, 2026. The transaction value is DKK 1.3 billion (about US$200 million), or DKK 1 billion post-tax. The completion follows the agreement first announced on July 22, 2026.

Certain inventories are being sold to JT with completion of the transaction; those proceeds are not included in the transaction value for the brands and will be counted in free cash flow before acquisitions and divestments. On that basis, STG raised its 2026 free-cash-flow guidance to a range of DKK 1.2–1.4 billion, revised from DKK 950 million to 1.2 billion.

Although the transaction is dilutive to earnings, STG said the divestment is not expected to affect its 2026 guidance for reported net sales growth at constant currencies, EBIT margin before special items or adjusted earnings per share, which are maintained. The exit from the two fine-cut brands continues the group's concentration on its cigar and pipe tobacco businesses.
