Por Larrañaga Intemporales, the Swiss Regional Edition 2023, Set for June Release

Habanos has announced a June 2026 release for the Por Larrañaga Intemporales, the Swiss Regional Edition 2023 — a three-year lag that underlines the regional program's supply delays.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
June 15, 2026
Release
June 2026 (announced by Cigar Journal on June 15, 2026)
Maker
Habanos S.A.

Habanos has announced that the Por Larrañaga Intemporales, the Swiss Regional Edition 2023, will be released in June 2026, Cigar Journal reported on June 15.

A cigar carrying a 2023 vintage that reaches shops only in the middle of 2026 is a telling case of the supply delays that have dogged Habanos’ regional program in recent years. The announcement, as carried by Cigar Journal, gave no details of the cigar’s size or pricing.

The Intemporales is also the latest entry in Switzerland’s regional-edition series — one of the longest-running in the program, stretching back to the Punch Robusto and Ramón Allones Eminencia of 2005.
