Trinidad Villa: a New LCDH-Exclusive Vitola Named for a World Heritage City

Unveiled in Mexico City on November 13, 2025, the Julieta No. 3 (47 x 170 mm) Villa is exclusive to La Casa del Habano and Habanos Specialists, in boxes of 12.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
November 13, 2025
Release
November 13, 2025
Maker
Habanos S.A.

Habanos S.A. has unveiled the Trinidad Villa, a new vitola created exclusively for the La Casa del Habano franchise network and Habanos Specialists, at a global launch held with Mexican distributor I.E.P.T. at Jardín Lambú in Mexico City on November 13, 2025. The Villa — factory name Julieta No. 3 (47 x 170 mm) — takes its name from the colonial villas of Trinidad, the Cuban city declared a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

The medium-strength blend is drawn from Vuelta Abajo leaves, and each cigar carries an additional band highlighting the concept as well as a foot band with the vitola name. The Villa is presented in a specially designed box of 12. More than 200 aficionados, distributors and guests attended the launch evening, where Habanos presented the cigar as an expression of Trinidad’s balanced house style, with notes of wood, toast and cocoa.
