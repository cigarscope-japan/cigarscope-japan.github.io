HVC Ships Hot Cake Fresh Out of the Oven Broadleaf Gladiators

HVC has begun shipping the Hot Cake Fresh Out of the Oven Broadleaf Gladiators, a 6 1/2 x 56 box-pressed perfecto and the eighth vitola in the limited series, limited to 500 boxes of 25.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
October 30, 2026
Release
Shipping began October 30, 2026
Maker
HVC Cigars

After a bit of a delay, HVC has begun shipping the Hot Cake Fresh Out of the Oven Broadleaf Gladiators, the eighth vitola in its limited Fresh Out of the Oven series, Halfwheel reported. The new cigar is a 6 1/2 x 56 box-pressed perfecto with a Connecticut broadleaf wrapper over Nicaraguan binder and filler from Estelí and Jalapa, rolled at Fábrica de Tabacos HVC.

The company showed the cigar in April during the 2026 PCA Convention & Trade Show and had hoped to ship it in the summer; shipping ultimately began on October 30. Fresh Out of the Oven is part of HVC's Pan Caliente ("hot cake") family of lines, and since the series debuted in 2024 it has added a succession of limited-edition vitolas, each limited in production.

The Gladiators is limited to 500 boxes of 25 cigars (12,500 cigars in total), priced at $15.20 per cigar and $380 per box. Separately, HVC said it is temporarily unable to access its social media accounts and encouraged retailers to contact the company by phone.
