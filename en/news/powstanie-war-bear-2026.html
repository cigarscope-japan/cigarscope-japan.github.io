Powstanie War Bear 2026 Box-Pressed Churchill Set for November 11 Release

A barber-pole 7 x 48 box-pressed Churchill from Nica Sueño, limited to 390 boxes of 21 at $16.50, named for Wojtek, the bear enlisted in the Polish army in the Second World War.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
November 5, 2026
Release
November 11, 2026
Maker
Powstanie Cigars

On November 11, Powstanie Cigars will release the War Bear 2026 as a 7 x 48 box-pressed Churchill. The date is Veterans Day in the United States, and the release plays into it: War Bear refers to Wojtek, a Syrian brown bear that was actually enlisted in the Polish army during the Second World War and is best known for moving crates of ammunition for Polish soldiers in 1944.

The cigar is a barber pole, combining two wrappers — Ecuadorian Habano and Mexican San Andrés — in an unbalanced design, over an Indonesian binder and fillers sourced from Nicaragua and the Dominican Republic. It is produced by Fábrica de Tabacos Nica Sueño S.A. in Estelí, Nicaragua, and is packaged in 21-count boxes, with total production limited to 390 boxes. Pricing is set at an SRP of $16.50 per cigar.

It is the second War Bear Powstanie has released this year, following the 250th American Anniversary War Bear released earlier in 2026.
