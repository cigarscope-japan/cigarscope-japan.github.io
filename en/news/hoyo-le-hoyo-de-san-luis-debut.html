Hoyo de Monterrey Debuts Le Hoyo de San Luis in Germany, a Vitola Rolled Only With San Luis Leaf

Habanos, S.A. unveiled the Hoyo de Monterrey Le Hoyo de San Luis (52 x 160 mm) in Germany today, November 12 — a medium-strength, totally handmade long-filler cigar rolled exclusively with leaf from San Luis in Vuelta Abajo, presented in boxes of 10 with pricing yet to be announced.

Habanos, S.A. has added a new vitola to Hoyo de Monterrey's Le Hoyo series, the Le Hoyo de San Luis, and gave it its global debut in Germany today, November 12. The launch took place at a dinner show at the Alexander Kunz Theatre, hosted by Fifth Avenue Products Trading GmbH, the exclusive distributor for Germany, Austria and Poland, and drew more than 400 guests including retailers, international media and aficionados.

The cigar measures 52 x 160 mm — its factory name is Fragancias — and is rated medium in strength. It is totally handmade with long filler, using leaves sourced exclusively from San Luis in Vuelta Abajo, Pinar del Río. Habanos describes the profile as woody and fresh in the first third, with toasted notes building through the middle and delicate cocoa notes appearing in the final third, over a smoking time of about an hour.

The Le Hoyo de San Luis is presented in boxes of 10. Pricing has not yet been announced.
