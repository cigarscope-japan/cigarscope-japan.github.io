Quai d'Orsay Concordia Edicion Limitada 2026 Premieres in Madrid

Only the second Limited Edition in the brand's history, the new Preciosos vitola (145 mm x 50) premiered at La Nuit D'Orsay in the Las Ventas bullring before more than 400 guests; boxes of 20 are priced at EUR 914.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
October 24, 2026
Release
World premiere October 24, 2026, in Madrid
Maker
Habanos S.A.
Share Post on X Share on LINE Facebook

Habanos S.A. has unveiled the Quai D'Orsay Concordia Edición Limitada 2026, only the second Limited Edition in the history of the brand — established in 1974 and synonymous with French culture and elegance — following the Senadores Limited Edition 2019. Named in honour of the Place de la Concorde in Paris, Concordia introduces a new vitola to the portfolio, the Preciosos, measuring 145 mm in length with a 50 ring gauge. In keeping with Limited Edition tradition, the wrapper, binder and filler leaves have been aged for at least two years, and the wrapper's extended fermentation gives it a deeper colour and oilier texture than the lighter tones traditionally associated with Quai D'Orsay.

Concordia is presented in a specially designed box of 20 cigars, at a recommended retail price of €914 per box — €45.70 per cigar, about $53. Each cigar carries the brand's primary band, an exclusive 2026 Limited Edition band and an additional foot band.

La Nuit D'Orsay at Las Ventas

The world premiere took place on October 24 in Madrid, during La Nuit D'Orsay at the Las Ventas bullring, organized by Club Pasión Habanos. More than 400 guests attended an evening styled on the Parisian Belle Époque, with gastronomy, music and pairings paying tribute to the brand's connection with France.
