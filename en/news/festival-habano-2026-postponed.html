26th Festival del Habano Postponed, Organizers Announce

Weeks before the Feb. 23-27 dates, the organizing committee postponed the festival to protect its standards of quality and experience; a new date was promised through official channels.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
February 14, 2026
Maker
Habanos S.A.

The Organizing Committee of the Habano Festival has announced the postponement of its next edition, Habanos S.A. said, “with the aim of preserving the highest standards of quality, excellence and experience that characterize this international event.” The 26th edition had been scheduled for February 23–27, 2026 in Havana and had earlier been reported sold out. “The priority of the Habano Festival is to offer its participants a comprehensive experience at the height of the relevance and prestige that this event represents internationally,” the organizers said.

The committee said it was working on setting a new date, to be announced through the festival's official channels. In a separate statement, Grupo Empresarial Tabacuba expressed regret for the postponement, which it said was “motivated by the complex economic situation facing the country as a result of the policy of intensifying the economic, commercial and financial blockade of the Government of the United States against Cuba.”
