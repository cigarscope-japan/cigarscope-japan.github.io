J.C. Newman Ships LeRoy Neiman 2026, With All Profits to Tampa Arts Education

The fourth LeRoy Neiman Collector's Edition is a 6 1/2 x 56 toro grande built around 'The President's Birthday,' limited to 750 boxes of 20 at $24, handmade at El Reloj in Tampa with an authentic lithograph in every box.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
May 29, 2026
Release
May 29, 2026
Maker
J.C. Newman

J.C. Newman has begun shipping the LeRoy Neiman 2026 Collector's Edition, the fourth installment in its collaboration with the LeRoy Neiman Foundation. This year's cigar is built around Neiman's "The President's Birthday," the painting of Marilyn Monroe serenading President John F. Kennedy on his birthday at Madison Square Garden in 1962; the cigars debuted at the 2026 PCA Convention & Trade Show in mid-April.

The cigar is a 6 1/2 x 56 toro grande with the same blend as the first three releases — a habano rosado wrapper of undisclosed origin, a Florida Sun Grown binder and aged filler tobaccos from Latin America — handmade at the company's El Reloj factory in Tampa. Each box contains an authentic lithograph of the painting, which also appears on one of the cigar's bands. The MSRP is $24 per cigar, and production is limited to 750 boxes of 20.

The release is tied to the 250th anniversary of the United States: "J.C. Newman is proud to roll official cigars of the 250th Anniversary of the United States," said Drew Newman, the company's fourth-generation owner. All profits from the collaboration are donated to arts education in Tampa's public schools, including those surrounding the El Reloj factory, and the project has raised $117,000 so far.
