Saint Luis Rey "Palatinos" Officially Announced: The Brand's First LCDH / Habanos Specialist Exclusive Regular Line

Habanos S.A. officially announced the Saint Luis Rey "Palatinos" on November 16 at a gala event in Macau. The brand's first regular-production vitola exclusive to La Casa del Habano and Habanos Specialist retailers, it is a Genios (5 1/2 x 52) in boxes of 20, full-bodied in the brand's tradition. Pricing has not been announced.

Habanos S.A.

According to Cigar Aficionado, Habanos S.A. officially announced the new Saint Luis Rey "Palatinos" on November 16 at a gala event in Macau. It is the brand's first-ever regular-production vitola exclusive to La Casa del Habano and Habanos Specialist retailers. The size is the Genios (5 1/2 x 52, 140mm), the same format as the Cohiba Maduro 5 Genios, presented in boxes of 20. The blend follows the brand's traditional full body — the official strength is "Fuerte" — using Vuelta Abajo binder and filler, all handmade. Pricing has not yet been announced. With more than 150 La Casa del Habano stores and more than 1,200 Habanos Specialists worldwide, distribution is expected to be wide. Saint Luis Rey was founded in 1940; until now only one regular-production size, the Regios, remained in the line. Our November 14 edition reported only the first unveiling in Macau; this official announcement confirms the size, box count, sales channel and blending direction.
