Smoke Inn Releases Black Works Studio Killer Bee Buzz Saw 2.0

The second Smoke Inn exclusive from Oveja Negra Brands recasts the Buzz Saw blend as a 5 1/4 x 54 box-pressed belicoso, sold in ten-count boxes at $120 and five-packs at $60.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
November 2, 2026
Release
November 2, 2026
Maker
Black Works Studio

Smoke Inn has announced its second exclusive cigar with Oveja Negra Brands, the Black Works Studio Killer Bee Buzz Saw 2.0. The new release introduces a new size of the blend, a 5 1/4 x 54 box-pressed belicoso, exclusive to the Florida retailer.

The Buzz Saw line dates back to Black Works Studio's 2021 Hive sampler, which featured special sizes from the Killer Bee and Hornet lines. In 2024, one of those sizes — a 5 x 56 perfecto — became a standalone release, the first Buzz Saw for Smoke Inn. The 2.0 changes the shape, from that rounded perfecto to the box-pressed belicoso.

Like the original, the Buzz Saw 2.0 uses a barber-pole wrapper of Ecuadorian maduro and Connecticut shade over Nicaraguan binder and fillers, and it is produced by Fabrica Oveja Negra in Estelí, Nicaragua. It comes in ten-count boxes priced at $120, and is also sold in five-packs at $60.
