Hoyo de Monterrey Marks 160 Years with a Spanish-Only Le Hoyo de Rio Seco Edition

For the brand's 160th anniversary, Habanos released a limited edition of Le Hoyo de Rio Seco, factory name Aromosos (140 mm x 56), exclusively in the Spanish market.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
March 2, 2026
Release
Announced March 2, 2026; released exclusively in the Spanish market
Maker
Habanos S.A.

Habanos is celebrating the 160th anniversary of Hoyo de Monterrey with a new limited edition of Le Hoyo de Río Seco, Cigar Journal reported on March 2. The cigar carries the factory name Aromosos and measures 140 mm with a 56 ring gauge.

The anniversary edition has been released exclusively in the Spanish market. That makes it an unusual form for a milestone release from one of Habanos' global brands: rather than a worldwide commemorative launch, the 160th anniversary of Hoyo de Monterrey is being marked with a single-market edition in Spain, historically one of the largest and most loyal markets for Cuban cigars.
