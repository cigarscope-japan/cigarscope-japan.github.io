Cohiba Behike BHK 58 Reaches Switzerland in the 15th Anniversary Selection Box

The largest Behike yet, 178 mm x 58, arrived at Swiss La Casa del Habano stores on Feb. 3 inside the four-cigar 15 Aniversario Selection box priced at CHF 1,696, with Habanos Specialists following on Feb. 10.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
January 22, 2026
Release
February 3, 2026
Maker
Habanos S.A.

The first Cohiba Behike BHK 58 cigars went on sale on February 3 at La Casa del Habano franchises in Switzerland, Intertabak AG, the distributor of Cuban cigars in Switzerland, announced, with Habanos Specialists across the country following from February 10, Cigars-connect reports. Presented at the 2025 Festival del Habano, the BHK 58 — at 178 mm x 58, the largest and most expensive of the regularly produced Habanos — is available only as part of the commemorative box celebrating the 15th anniversary of the Behike line, the same box given to attendees of the Festival's gala evening in February 2025.

The Cohiba BHK 15 Aniversario Selection gift set contains one each of the BHK 52, BHK 54, BHK 56 and BHK 58, at a suggested retail price of CHF 1,696 (€1,825, about $2,150). In the set, the cigars carry a band commemorating the line's 15th anniversary as well as a foot band. Because the set is priced as a package, the price of the BHK 58 alone is difficult to determine.
