S.T. Dupont Opens the Cohiba 60th Anniversary Collection, Lighters from €690

The first part of S.T. Dupont's Cohiba 60th anniversary collection launched on June 8 — three lighters (€690 to €2,300), each limited to 1,966 pieces, with a second instalment due in November.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
June 8, 2026
Release
June 8, 2026
Maker
Habanos S.A.

S.T. Dupont has officially launched the accessories collection it created in collaboration with Habanos S.A. to celebrate the 60th anniversary of Cohiba, Cuba’s most prestigious brand. The launch comes in two stages, Cigars-connect reported: the first part was unveiled on Monday, June 8, 2026, and the rest will follow in November. The two houses traditionally present their collaborations during the Festival del Habano, but this year’s event was postponed indefinitely due to the energy crisis affecting the island.

The first chapter of the collection offers lighters, an ashtray, a cigar cutter, a cigar case holding three cigars and a pair of cufflinks. The lighters lead the range: a table lighter with dual ignition — soft flame and torch — at €690, its black lacquered body bearing the anniversary logo of the Taíno head between the intertwined numbers 6 and 0; a Ligne 2 soft-flame model at €2,000, in which the Taíno head pierces the entire thickness of the lighter; and the Le Grand Dupont, also with double ignition, at €2,300. Each of these models is limited to 1,966 pieces, a nod to the year the Cohiba brand was founded, and their edges reproduce the Cohiba checkerboard pattern with a guilloché relief effect.

Above the main range sits the “Haute Création” workshop: a “Complication Jackpot” lighter, a miniature slot machine whose reels display combinations such as CO-HI-BA, MA-DU-RO and BE-HI-KE (no price announced), and gold versions of the Le Grand Dupont and Ligne 2, with diamonds for the checkerboard pattern.
