EGM Begins Shipping Ninfa Jars, 50 Slender Cigars in a Numbered Ceramic Jar

EGM Cigars has begun shipping the Ninfa Jar, a handmade Italian ceramic jar holding 50 of its slender 7 x 34 Ninfas, limited to 500 numbered jars at $3,000 each.

Published
November 10, 2026
Announced
November 2, 2026
Release
Shipping began November 2026; arriving at U.S. retailers this week (reported November 2, 2026)
Maker
EGM Cigars

EGM Cigars has begun shipping its Ninfa Jars, a new limited-edition packaging option for its core line that debuted at the 2026 PCA Convention & Trade Show in April, Halfwheel reported. Inside each jar are 50 "specially selected" Ninfas, a long, slender size measuring 7 x 34 that is rarely seen in the premium cigar world. The jars should be arriving at U.S. stores this week.

The blend is unchanged from the version sold in boxes: all-Dominican wrapper, binder and filler, with the tobacco aged for at least three years — seco-dominant, with some ligero added. The cigars are made at ABAM Cigars S.R.L. in the Dominican Republic. "Ninfas are a dream come true," said Ettore Moraschinelli of EGM Cigars. "These elegant cigars speak to us about a bygone era. It's a size that asks you to slow down and listen to the leaf."

The ceramic jars are handmade in Italy and individually numbered, with production limited to 500 pieces. In the United States, each jar carries an MSRP of $3,000, and the jars will be sold through the company's retail partners only — not online.
