Aladino 85th Anniversary Elegante: Price and Allocation Confirmed at $22, Two Boxes a Month

Pricing and sales terms have been confirmed for the Elegante, the new 7 x 38 lancero in Aladino's 85 Aniversario Reserva line. The cigar uses the Eiroa family's own-grown Pinareno tobacco for wrapper, binder and filler, comes in 20-count boxes at $22 a cigar, and is limited to Aladino Reserva retailers carrying the line's Robusto and Toro, with an allocation of two boxes per store per month. It is already shipping.

Aladino has confirmed the price and sales terms for the Elegante, the new size in its 85 Aniversario Reserva blend. The lancero, 7 x 38, uses the Eiroa family's own-grown Pinareno tobacco for wrapper, binder and filler alike. It comes in 20-count boxes at $22 a cigar. The cigar's shipping debut was reported earlier this month; the price and allocation terms are the newly confirmed details.

Sales are limited to Aladino Reserva retailers that carry the 85th Aniversario Robusto and Toro, with an allocation of two boxes per store per month. The Elegante is made at Fabrica de Puros Aladino in Honduras and is already shipping.
