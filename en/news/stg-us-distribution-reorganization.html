STG Shuffles Its U.S. Portfolio: Cohiba and Punch Move to Forged, Room101 to General

Scandinavian Tobacco Group is swapping five brands between its two U.S. sales divisions from February 2, 2026, a move meant to leave General Cigar and Forged Cigar roughly equal in size under the new Focus2030 strategy.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
January 14, 2026
Maker
Scandinavian Tobacco Group
Share Post on X Share on LINE Facebook

Scandinavian Tobacco Group (STG) is making changes to its two U.S. sales divisions, swapping five brands between them. Most notably, the non-Cuban Cohiba brand will now be sold by Forged Cigar Co. instead of General Cigar Co.; Punch and Havana Honeys move to Forged as well, while Partagas and Room101 move in the opposite direction, from Forged to General.

The new line-ups take effect on February 2, 2026. Collectively, the changes are aimed at making Forged and General equal divisions in terms of sales, as part of STG's Focus2030 campaign that is now kicking off, and Forged is expected to expand its sales team. The two divisions are a rare set-up in the cigar business: both are owned by STG and share factories, marketing teams and executives, yet their individual sales representatives compete directly against each other for the same retailers.

As part of the changes, STG has removed several brands from the price lists — Brioso, Honduran Bundles, La Estrella Cubana, Los Statos Deluxe, Room101 Farce and Sancho Panza. The first three are moving to the portfolio of Meier & Dutch, the wholesale arm of Cigars International that serves as STG's third U.S. division; the company has not said what will happen to the other brands.
