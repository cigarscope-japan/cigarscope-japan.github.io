Two New Habanos Specialist Stores Take Vietnam's Network to 14

New Habanos Specialist stores in Ho Chi Minh City and Nha Trang have taken Vietnam's network to 14 stores, in an expansion led by Avanti Group with exclusive distributor Golden Phoenix Vietnam.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
April 28, 2026
Maker
Habanos S.A.

Two new Habanos Specialist stores have opened in Ho Chi Minh City and Nha Trang, bringing the total number of Habanos Specialist stores in Vietnam to 14, Habanos S.A. announced. The openings cap six years of steady development since Avanti Group opened the country’s first Habanos Specialist.

The expansion is led by Avanti Group, under its general director Sami Alrayhani, in collaboration with Golden Phoenix Vietnam, the exclusive distributor of Habanos brands in the country, directed by Thuy Thu Van. In Ho Chi Minh City, the new store unfolds across three levels and 400 m², centred on a 40 m² walk-in humidor and completed by a private room, a cigar lounge and a terrace overlooking the Saigon River.

The Nha Trang store, at Ariyana Nha Trang on the central coast, spans over 500 m² and is the largest Habanos Specialist in Central Vietnam. Alongside the full Habanos portfolio, it carries houses such as S.T. Dupont.
