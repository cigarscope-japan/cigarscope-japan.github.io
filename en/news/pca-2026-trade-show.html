PCA26 Wraps in New Orleans: 262 Exhibitors and More Than 5,945 Attendees

The Premium Cigar Association's PCA26 ran April 17-20 in New Orleans with 262 exhibiting companies, about 2,540 retailers from 970 accounts and total attendance above 5,945.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
April 30, 2026
Maker
Premium Cigar Association (PCA)

The Premium Cigar Association has concluded its annual convention and trade show, PCA26, held April 17–20, 2026, in New Orleans. The show drew 262 exhibiting companies and nearly 2,540 retailers from 970 retail accounts, with more than 5,945 total attendees across the four days.

The event opened on April 17 with a full day of seminars, panels and the PCA Annual Meeting before the trade show floor officially opened on Saturday. Highlights included the debut of a new edition of the film Hand Rolled, this time focusing on the Padrón family; the presentation of the Best of the Leaf and Advocacy Awards; author Nick Hammond previewing his work in conversation with the Newman family; and a fireside chat with Marlon Wayans and Guy Fieri. Local television and news media also covered the gathering.

"The energy on the show floor, the quality of business being done, and the enthusiasm from our members made it clear that this was one of the most impactful trade shows in recent history," said Joshua Habursky, the PCA's CEO. Association president Todd Naifeh added: "Retailers came ready to buy, exhibitors came ready to innovate, and the result was a trade show that created real momentum for the year ahead."
