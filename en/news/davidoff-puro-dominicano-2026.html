Davidoff Brings Back Puro Dominicano as a Regular Line, All-Dominican in Three Sizes

The 2009 limited edition's name returns with a new blend made entirely of Dominican tobacco from six regions, aged a combined 32 years, in three sizes that joined the Black Band Collection on April 23.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
March 12, 2026
Release
April 23, 2026
Maker
Davidoff

The Davidoff Puro Dominicano is returning in 2026, this time as a regular production line rather than a limited edition. The name was first used in 2009 for a 5 3/4 x 48 belicoso; the new version carries a new blend, a Davidoff spokesperson confirmed, and new black-band packaging.

As the name promises, the cigar is made entirely of Dominican tobacco. Davidoff has not itemised the leaves, but says they come from six different regions of the country and have been aged for a combined 32 years. Three sizes are offered: a Short Robusto (4 1/2 x 52, $19.80), a Corona Larga (5 7/8 x 44, $23.30) and a Perfecto (5 1/2 x 50, $25.20), each sold in packs of five and boxes of 12.

Puro Dominicano becomes the fourth pillar of Davidoff's Black Band Collection, alongside Escurio, Nicaragua and Yamasá. "With Puro Dominicano, we are strengthening the foundation of our Black Band Collection by highlighting the Dominican Republic's exceptional tobacco heritage," said Javier González, the company's svp head of global marketing and innovation. The cigars began arriving on shelves on April 23, 2026.
