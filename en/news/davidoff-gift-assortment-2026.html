Davidoff Packages AVO, Baccarat, Camacho and Zino in a 10-Count Gift Assortment

Davidoff has informed retailers of a 10-count Gift Assortment gathering toro sizes from AVO, Baccarat, Camacho and Zino at a suggested retail price of $90 — $9 per cigar — aimed at the holiday season.

Announced

Announced to retailers November 10, 2026
Maker
Davidoff

Davidoff has begun informing retailers about a 10-count Gift Assortment that gathers toro sizes from four of its non-Davidoff brands — AVO, Baccarat, Camacho and Zino — Cigar Coop reported. The suggested retail price is $90, which works out to $9 per cigar, positioning the set as a holiday-season value pack.

The assortment contains the AVO Clasic No. 2, AVO XO Legato and AVO Syncro Nicaragua Toro; the Baccarat The Game; the Zino Nicaragua Toro and Zino Honduras Toro; and four Camachos — the Connecticut Toro, Ecuador Toro, Corojo Toro and Triple Maduro Gordo. All of the cigars are toro-sized except the Triple Maduro, which comes as a gordo.
