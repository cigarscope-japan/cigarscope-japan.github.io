Black Works Studio Announces Shaolin 36th Chamber, a Tri-Barber Pole Limited Edition Shipping Late November

Black Works Studio will ship the Shaolin 36th Chamber to select retailers later this month — a 5 3/4 x 46 petite belicoso in 24-count boxes at $13.50 per cigar, wearing an Ecuadorian habano, maduro and candela tri-barber pole wrapper.

Black Works Studio (BLK WKS) will ship the Shaolin 36th Chamber, a new limited edition, to select retailers later this month, Cigar Coop reported. The single size is a 5 3/4 x 46 petite belicoso in 24-count boxes, priced at $13.50 per cigar ($324 per box). It is handcrafted in Estelí, Nicaragua, at Fabrica Oveja Negra.

The blend wears a tri-barber pole wrapper — Ecuadorian habano with maduro and candela accents — over a Nicaraguan habano binder with Nicaraguan fillers. First showcased at the 2026 Premium Cigar Association trade show, its annual BLK WKS limited series pays homage to classic kung fu movies: chambers 1 through 35 were for the monks, founder James Brown explained, while the 36th chamber was for the people.

Brown described the flavour profile as "herbal tea, citrus, and bread notes," with light spice up front, medium strength and a deliberate move away from heavier cigars toward approachability.
