Bolivar Safari, the First Regional Edition for the Whole of Africa, Debuts November 21

A first for the Edicion Regional programme, the Bolivar Safari is a 54 x 111 mm Torres with a pigtail, debuting on November 21 in Abidjan in 16,666 numbered boxes of 12 at roughly 43 euros.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
October 13, 2026
Release
November 21, 2026
Maker
Habanos S.A.

Cuba has been making Regional Edition cigars for more than 20 years, for countries and territories around the world — including, in the past, South Africa. But there has never been one for the entire continent of Africa. That changes next month with the release of the Bolívar Safari, Cigar Aficionado reported.

The Safari is short but plump: 4 3/8 inches long, the length of a petit corona, with a thick 54 ring gauge — a format known as a Torres in Cuban factories, the same size as the Trinidad Vigía. Like the Vigía, the Safaris carry pigtails, a first for the Bolívar brand, according to Phoenicia Trading A.A., the Cyprus-based company that distributes Cuban cigars in Africa. The cigars come in individually numbered boxes of 12, with a run of 16,666 boxes — just shy of 200,000 cigars — and are expected to retail for roughly €43 per smoke, or about $48.

Sales will begin with a launch party on November 21 in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire, thrown by Phoenicia and its local partner Zino Abidjan. The Safari will first be sold throughout Côte d'Ivoire from November 21 before expanding to other parts of Africa in December.
