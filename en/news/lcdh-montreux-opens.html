Switzerland's Tenth La Casa del Habano Opens Inside a Five-Star Hotel in Montreux

Intertabak and Habanos S.A. inaugurated Switzerland's tenth La Casa del Habano on March 19 — the country's first inside a five-star palace hotel, at the Fairmont Montreux Palace on the shores of Lake Geneva.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
March 19, 2026
Maker
Habanos S.A.

Switzerland’s tenth La Casa del Habano opened on March 19, 2026, in Montreux, on the shores of Lake Geneva. Intertabak AG and Habanos S.A., in collaboration with Montabaco S.A., inaugurated the boutique inside the Fairmont Montreux Palace — the first La Casa del Habano in Switzerland located within a five-star palace hotel.

The Monbaron family led the inauguration, which was held in the presence of representatives of Intertabak, the Habanos distributor for Switzerland, and of Laura Ivet Pujol Torres, Cuba’s ambassador to the Swiss Confederation. Montreux joins the established Casas of Basel, Samnaun, St. Gallen, Zug and Zurich in German-speaking Switzerland, Lugano and Mendrisio in Ticino, and Geneva and Nyon in the French-speaking part of the country.

Like the other Casas, the boutique stocks special editions, limited series and specialties exclusive to the franchise network. An exclusive smoking lounge completes the offer, together with accessories and premium spirits. The store, at Avenue Claude Nobs 2, opens Tuesday to Saturday.
