Tatuaje Skinny Michael Scores 91 — Only "The Bride" Remains Without a Skinny Version

Halfwheel has reviewed the Tatuaje Skinny Michael, the skinny version of The Michael (2017) from the Monster Series, and scored it 91 points. The 6 x 38 petit lancero uses the same blend as the original — an Ecuadorian habano wrapper over Nicaraguan double binder and fillers — and is made by My Father Cigars. It shipped in October 2026 alongside the Skinny Krueger at $9.50 per cigar and $237.50 per 25-count box. Of the 13 original Monsters, The Bride is now the only one without a skinny version; the Monster Series has reached 106 releases in total.

According to Halfwheel, the Tatuaje Skinny Michael — the skinny version of The Michael (2017), the Monster Series cigar named for Michael Myers of the film "Halloween" — has been reviewed and scored 91 points. The 6 x 38 petit lancero uses the same blend as the original, an Ecuadorian habano wrapper over Nicaraguan double binder and fillers, and is made by My Father Cigars. Halfwheel found it a refreshing smoke led by the Ecuadorian habano, medium-plus in flavor and body and mild in strength.

The Skinny Michael shipped in October 2026 alongside the Skinny Krueger, at $9.50 per cigar and $237.50 per 25-count box. With its release, The Bride is the only one of the 13 original Monsters still without a skinny version, and the Monster Series has reached 106 releases in total.
