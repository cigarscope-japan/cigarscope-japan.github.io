Trinidad Vigia Reserva Cosecha 2022 Is the Brand's First Reserva

Trinidad's first Reserva uses leaves aged at least three extra years in the 54 x 110 mm Vigia vitola — 5,000 numbered boxes of 20 at $3,800, 5,000 gift boxes of three at $660, and an NFC chip in the band.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
September 23, 2026
Release
World premiere September 19, 2026, in Montreux; retail release date not announced
Maker
Habanos S.A.

Habanos S.A. has unveiled the Trinidad Vigía Reserva Cosecha 2022, the first Reserva in the history of Trinidad, a brand created in 1969 whose name pays tribute to the historic city of Trinidad. The Reserva is presented in the Vigía vitola — at 54 ring gauge x 110 mm, the largest ring gauge in the brand's current portfolio — with a medium-strength profile, made from leaves that have undergone an additional aging process of at least three years.

Production is limited to 5,000 individually numbered boxes of 20 cigars, at a recommended retail price of $3,800 (€3,276) per box, plus 5,000 gift boxes of three cigars at $660 (€570). The distinctive Reserva band carries NFC technology, allowing aficionados to access product information and verify the authenticity of each cigar.

World premiere in Montreux

The global unveiling took place on September 19 at the Petit Palais of the Fairmont Montreux Palace in Montreux, Switzerland, at an event organized with Intertabak, the exclusive Habanos distributor for the Swiss market, before 300 international guests. The evening's highlight was a pairing of the new Reserva with The Dalmore King Alexander III.
