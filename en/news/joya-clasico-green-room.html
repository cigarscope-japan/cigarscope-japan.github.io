Joya de Nicaragua's Clásico Original Green Room Selection Is a Limited Diadema Named for a White House Parlour

Joya de Nicaragua is adding a 6 x 54 diadema to its Clásico Original line, wrapped in the same Ecuadorian Connecticut-seed shade leaf as Número Uno. The 1,200-box release takes its name from the White House Green Room, where guests once gathered over drinks and cigars.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
March 18, 2026
Release
Debuts at PCA 2026 (April 18-20, 2026); shipping to stores shortly after the show
Maker
Joya de Nicaragua

Joya de Nicaragua is adding a new vitola to one of its oldest lines. The Clásico Original Green Room Selection is a 6 x 54 diadema made with a shade-grown Ecuadorian Connecticut-seed wrapper over a Nicaraguan binder and Nicaraguan fillers. The company says the wrapper is the same as the one used on its highly rated Número Uno line, though the two blends differ inside.

In the U.S., the cigar will have an MSRP of $13.50 and will be sold in 20-count boxes. Production is limited to 1,200 boxes in total: 700 are allocated to U.S. retailers and the remaining 500 are reserved for international markets. The Green Room Selection will debut during the 2026 PCA Convention & Trade Show, held April 18-20 in New Orleans, and is expected to ship to stores shortly after the trade show concludes.

Joya has made the Clásico line for most of its nearly 60-year history, and in the 1970s it became the official cigar of the Nixon White House. The new cigar's name refers to the White House's Green Room, a first-floor parlour where guests would gather to enjoy drinks and cigars. "A cigar has the unique ability to bring people together and make everyone equal for a moment," said Juan Martínez, the company's executive president. "Every smoker eventually creates his own Green Room."
