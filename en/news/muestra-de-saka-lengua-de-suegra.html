Dunbarton's Muestra de Saka Lengua de Suegra Is the Line's First Culebra

Dunbarton Tobacco & Trust is adding its first culebra to the Muestra de Saka series: Lengua de Suegra braids three 7 x 40 cigars of the Mi Querida blend — rolled with long filler, against culebra convention — and ships in October in coffins at $60.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
April 15, 2026
Release
October 2026 (expected shipping)
Maker
Dunbarton Tobacco & Trust

At the 2026 Premium Cigar Association (PCA) trade show, Dunbarton Tobacco & Trust will introduce the latest instalment of its Muestra de Saka series, Lengua de Suegra — the company's first culebra offering. The name translates to "mother-in-law's tongue" and refers to the spider plant, also known as the snake plant.

Lengua de Suegra uses the Mi Querida blend, which consists of Connecticut broadleaf over Nicaraguan binder and filler. Although it consists of three cigars braided together, the component cigars are 7 x 40 — a size bigger than most culebras. Master ligador Steve Saka said he used long filler for the blend, noting that culebras are in many cases made with short filler to make braiding the cigars together easier. Production comes from Nicaraguan American Cigars, S.A. (NACSA).

Each three-cigar braid will be sold in its own coffin, at an SRP of $60 per coffin, with three coffins per box. Lengua de Suegra is expected to ship in October.
