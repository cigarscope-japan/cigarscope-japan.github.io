Spain's 2026 Cuban Cigar Prices Rise a Modest 3.83% on Average

Halfwheel's comparison of Spain's roughly 350-SKU price list finds the premium lines and Trinidad held flat, a sharp slowdown from the increases of 2023 and 2025, with the Cohiba Ideales the dearest cigar at EUR 550.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
January 14, 2026
Maker
Habanos S.A.
Share Post on X Share on LINE Facebook

Cuban cigars in Spain are getting a modest price increase for 2026, Halfwheel reports. Comparing the January 1, 2026 price list with the January 3, 2025 list across roughly 350 SKUs, prices rose by an average of 3.83 percent — a second straight year of modest increases after an estimated 8.23 percent in 2023 and about 5 percent in 2025, and a sharp contrast with 2022, when some prices doubled or tripled overnight under Habanos S.A.'s “global pricing standard.” Spain is a useful bellwether: with low taxes it is one of the least expensive places to buy Cuban cigars, and in 2025 it was the second-largest market for them after China.

The clearest change is at the top of the range. The expensive sub-brands — Cohiba Behike BHK, Montecristo Línea 1935, Partagás Línea Maestra and Romeo y Julieta Línea de Oro — are unaffected by the January 1 change, and Trinidad prices are unchanged for the third year in a row apart from a €0.05 rise for the Trinidad Short. Nineteen Cohibas on the list now cost at least €100 per cigar; the most expensive is the Cohiba Ideales at €550 (about $640). The largest percentage rise was the Quai d'Orsay Imperiales, up 56.06 percent to €72.10 after a second increase during 2025.
