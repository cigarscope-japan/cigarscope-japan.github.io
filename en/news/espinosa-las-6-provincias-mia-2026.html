Espinosa's Las 6 Provincias MIA Arrives at Stores — Final Chapter Honors Miami, Limited to 20,000 Cigars

The seventh and final release in Espinosa Premium Cigars' limited "Las 6 Provincias" line, "MIA," has begun arriving at stores. The series is named for the six provinces of Cuba at independence; this final chapter is an homage to Miami, the unofficial seventh province. It is a Nicaraguan puro made at the San Lotano factory, in a 6 1/4 x 55 toro extra, at $18 per cigar and $360 per 20-count box. Production is limited to 1,000 boxes of 20 (20,000 cigars). According to Halfwheel, the seventh and final release in Espinosa Premium Cigars' limited "Las 6 Provincias" line, "MIA," has begun arriving at stores. The series is named for the six provinces of Cuba at independence; this final chapter is an homage to Miami, the unofficial seventh province. It is a Nicaraguan puro made at the San Lotano factory, in a 6 1/4 x 55 toro extra. Each cigar is $18, with 20-count boxes at $360. Production is limited to 1,000 boxes of 20 (20,000 cigars).

The cigar was unveiled at the PCA trade show in April 2026 and released on November 19, 2026. The previous six releases in the series came out between 2018 and 2024, each in runs of around 1,000 boxes.
