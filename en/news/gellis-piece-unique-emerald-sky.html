Gellis Family Cigars Adds Piece Unique Emerald Sky, Limited to 3,000 Cigars

The second Piece Unique of 2026 is a 5 1/2 x 48 one-off blend with rare Peruvian pelo d'oro in the filler, priced at $40 and scheduled to ship in late November.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
October 30, 2026
Release
Late November 2026 (boxes scheduled to ship to retailers)
Maker
Gellis Family Cigars

Gellis Family Cigars — a brand started by Kyle Gellis, the owner of Warped Cigars — will be releasing the Piece Unique Emerald Sky, a 5 1/2 x 48 cigar with an Ecuadorian Connecticut-seed wrapper over a Mexican San Andrés binder and filler tobaccos from the Dominican Republic and Nicaragua. The filler also includes pelo d'oro tobacco from Peru, a rare heirloom variety appearing in the series for the first time. The cigar is made at Tabacalera La Isla in the Dominican Republic.

It is the second Piece Unique scheduled for 2026, following the Piece Unique 2026, a 6 x 52 toro with a similar blend on paper but pink packaging, where the Emerald Sky uses green. "Piece unique" is a watch term, typically referring to a one-of-one watch; here, the idea is that each specific blend will only be made once. The line debuted in 2024, sold to U.S. stores, while the 2025 release was offered only to non-U.S. retailers.

Production is limited to 3,000 cigars, in boxes of ten, priced at $40 per cigar and $400 per box. Boxes are scheduled to ship to retailers in late November.
