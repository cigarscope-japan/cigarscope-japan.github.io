La Gloria Cubana Série Brasil No. 3: Brazil's Third Regional Edition

The campana-format (52 x 140 mm) Série Brasil No. 3 arrives in 6,000 numbered boxes of 10 at an average of EUR 490, and brings the La Gloria Cubana brand itself to Brazil.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
June 16, 2026
Release
June 16, 2026
Maker
Habanos S.A.

Brazil’s third Habanos Regional Edition, the La Gloria Cubana Série Brasil No. 3, is now available at the country’s main shops, Cigar Journal reports. Presented last year during the Habanos Night, the campana (52 x 140 mm) comes in a limited series of 6,000 individually numbered boxes of 10; Brazil does not regulate cigar retail prices, and the box is selling at an average of €490 (about $570).

The release also introduces the La Gloria Cubana brand to the Brazilian market: Emporium Cigars, the official Habanos distributor for Brazil, will now carry the brand’s regular-production vitolas alongside the “Exclusivo Brasil” edition. Brazil’s two earlier regional editions — the Bolívar Redentores, presented in 2013, and the El Rey del Mundo Série BN2 Brasil of 2021 — are both sold out.
