My Father Marks 20 Years of the Don Pepín García Clásico With a Limited Toro Extra

My Father is celebrating the 20th anniversary of the Don Pepín García Cuban Classic — the black-band line that, unlike its Miami-made siblings, has been made in Nicaragua since its 2006 debut — with a 6 1/2 x 52 toro extra limited to 2,500 boxes, each with a cutter.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
April 11, 2026
Release
2026, the line's 20th anniversary year; pricing and a specific release date had not been announced
Maker
My Father Cigars

Before there was My Father Cigars, most of the cigars sold by the García family used the name of the family patriarch: Don Pepín García. Those lines included the original Don Pepín García, which had a blue band; Series JJ, with white bands; and Cuban Classic, which used a black band. By 2006, when Cuban Classic was introduced, the family's El Rey de los Habanos was already having trouble keeping up with production at its small Miami factory — so unlike the other Don Pepín García-branded cigars, Cuban Classic has always been made in Nicaragua.

This year, as My Father celebrates 20 years of Cuban Classic, it is deliberately paying tribute to that fact with the new Don Pepín García Clásico 20th Anniversary. As with many of the company's anniversary cigars, it comes in a 6 1/2 x 52 toro extra size. No details have been given about the blend, but the company's social media posts carry a caption that reads, "Made in Nicaragua to honor 20 years of the original."

The release is limited to 2,500 boxes of 20 cigars, and each box will come with a single guillotine cutter. Pricing and a release date had not been announced when the cigar was unveiled ahead of the 2026 PCA Convention & Trade Show, held April 18-20 in New Orleans.
