Trinidad x Tommy Bahama Adds Four Accessories and a Camp Shirt — Chapter Two of the Island Collection

Altadis U.S.A. has expanded its Trinidad x Tommy Bahama Island Collection with four accessories and a camp shirt, one year after the cigar collection debuted. The desktop humidor is $275, the swivel torch lighter $99, the travel case $158 and the ashtray $75, all on sale now at cigar retailers across the United States; the silk camp shirt "One Puff Closer to Paradise" is $195 for the holiday season.

Published
November 15, 2026
Announced
November 13, 2026
Maker
Altadis U.S.A.

Altadis U.S.A. has added four accessories and a camp shirt to the Island Collection, its Trinidad x Tommy Bahama collaboration, in a second chapter arriving one year after the cigar collection's debut. The accessories are a desktop humidor at $275, a swivel torch lighter at $99, a travel case at $158 and an ashtray at $75, and they are already on sale at cigar retailers across the United States. Selected Tommy Bahama stores and the brand's official website also carry them (Smokin Tabacco also reported the launch on November 14).

The silk camp shirt, called "One Puff Closer to Paradise," is $195 and will be sold this holiday season at selected Tommy Bahama stores and on the official website. Oliver Hyams, Altadis U.S.A.'s vice president of marketing, says Tommy Bahama's sophisticated, relaxed attitude is a natural fit for the Trinidad brand.
