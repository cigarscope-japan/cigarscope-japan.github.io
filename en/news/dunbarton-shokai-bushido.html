Dunbarton's Shokai Bushido Debuts at Cigartopia on November 14, Born of the Takumi Project

Steve Saka will unveil Shokai Bushido, a one-off Cigar Dojo collaboration for Cigars Daily, at Cigartopia in Phoenix on November 14 — a cigar that spun out of Takumi, the very limited blend he has worked on for three years.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
November 3, 2026
Release
November 14, 2026
Maker
Dunbarton Tobacco & Trust

Dunbarton Tobacco & Trust founder Steve Saka has spent roughly three years developing a new blend called Takumi, and in a Facebook post this week he offered fresh detail: Takumi will use an Ecuadorian Sumatra-seed wrapper, is planned as a very limited production liga, and may be showcased at the PCA 2027 trade show in March. The cigar itself is finished, Saka said, though the remaining work behind a release is still under way. Dunbarton filed a trademark application for the Takumi name in July.

Takumi's development also produced a second cigar. Shokai Bushido — a name Saka translates as "an introduction to the way of the warrior" — was not exactly what he wanted for Takumi, but he describes it as a tremendously tasty smoke in its own right. Developed in collaboration with the online community Cigar Dojo, it is a one-off release for Cigars Daily in the mould of Saka's earlier store-exclusive drops.

Shokai Bushido will debut at the Cigartopia 2026 event, held at Cigars Daily's headquarters in Phoenix on November 14. The cigars will be sold in person at the event first, with any remaining stock offered through the Cigars Daily website. They come in 10-count bundles; Saka said he is still working out pricing, and production numbers, the producing factory and other blend details have not been disclosed.
