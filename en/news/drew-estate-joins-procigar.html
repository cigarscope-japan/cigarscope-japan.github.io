Drew Estate Joins ProCigar as Drew Dominicana Restores the Association to 12 Full Members

Drew Estate has joined ProCigar through its new Drew Dominicana factory, returning the Dominican manufacturers' association to 12 full members — an uncommon pre-opening membership granted before the factory starts production.

Published
November 11, 2026
Announced
November 10, 2026
Maker
Drew Estate

Drew Estate has joined ProCigar, the association of Dominican cigar manufacturers, through its new Drew Dominicana factory — returning the organisation to 12 full members, Halfwheel reported. Cigar Aficionado carried the same news the same day. Membership normally requires a company to be actively selling cigars made in the Dominican Republic; Drew Estate was admitted in advance of the factory opening, an uncommon pre-opening membership.

Membership carries visible privileges: boxes coming out of Drew Dominicana will bear the ProCigar sticker, and the factory is expected to join the factory tours of the ProCigar Festival, held each February in Santiago.

ProCigar began 2026 with 12 full members before falling to 10, a drop that included Davidoff's departure. Drew Dominicana is Drew Estate's first owned factory outside Nicaragua, where its production — including its infused lines — has been concentrated until now.
