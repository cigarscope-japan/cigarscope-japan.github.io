Plasencia Announces 2026 Holiday Collection: Five Toros From Five Lines

The five-count set draws one toro each from Reserva Original, Alma del Fuego, Alma del Cielo, Alma Fuerte and Alma de Campo, in a snowflake-embossed green box at $99.99, shipping in November.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
November 4, 2026
Release
November 2026 (shipping during the month)
Maker
Plasencia Cigars

Plasencia Cigars has announced a new Holiday Collection for 2026, a five-count unit featuring five Toro-sized cigars drawn from across the company's lines: the Reserva Original Toro, the Alma del Fuego Concepción, the Alma del Cielo Boreal, the Alma Fuerte Néstor IV and the Alma de Campo Travesía in its box-pressed form.

The collection comes in a deep green box embossed with snowflakes and gold accents, with a display window showcasing all five cigars. "This collection brings together five distinct expressions of the family's passion for tobacco and craftsmanship," said Ed Bello, chief executive officer of Plasencia Cigars. "It is a thoughtful way to celebrate the holidays, discover the diversity of our portfolio, and share the Plasencia experience with others."

Pricing for the 2026 Holiday Collection is set at an SRP of $99.99 per five-count unit, and the collection is shipping to authorized retailers during November.
