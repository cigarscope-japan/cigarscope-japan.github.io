Twenty Years of 'Exclusivo Suiza': Switzerland's Regional Editions

Switzerland has commissioned a Habanos Regional Edition almost every year since the program began in 2005 — from the 2005 Punch Robusto and Ramón Allones Eminencia to the delayed 2023 Por Larrañaga Intemporales.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
July 21, 2023
Maker
Habanos S.A.

The Edición Regional program, introduced by Habanos S.A. in 2005, allows official distributors to commission exclusive productions for their own markets — and Switzerland has taken part from the very first year. Under the program’s rules, set out in La Casa del Habano Nyon’s explainer, a distributor commits to ordering a minimum of 25,000 cigars and pays production costs in advance; the cigars are then sold exclusively in that territory for one year, with a possible second year as an identical reissue. Each cigar wears a second band, always red with silver lettering, bearing the word “Exclusivo” followed by the name of the region.

Switzerland’s first editions, in 2005, were the Punch Robusto and the Ramón Allones Eminencia, each in boxes of 25. Their original second bands read simply “Edición Regional,” without the country designation used today, and the Eminencia proved popular enough to be re-released in 2007 with the newer style of band — Halfwheel records 1,221 boxes shipped for the 2007 re-release.

The Swiss list has grown steadily since: the Bolívar Legendarios (2008), the Bolívar Presidente (2013), the Ramón Allones Perfectos (2014 — a 48 x 137 mm perfecto in 5,000 boxes of 10), the Quai d’Orsay Selection Royale (2020 — 50 x 184 mm, produced for the 25th anniversary of Swiss distributor Intertabak AG in 1,600 boxes of 50) and the Diplomáticos El Emisario (2021). With the Por Larrañaga Intemporales, the delayed Swiss edition of 2023, the series now counts twenty editions across two decades.
