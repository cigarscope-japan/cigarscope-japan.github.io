STG Drops Hoyo Epicure Seleccion and Ten More Items From Its Price List

STG's new price lists bring almost no price increases — a few items moved by pennies — but remove 11 products from the General Cigar Co. list, led by the Hoyo de Monterrey Epicure Seleccion line.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
November 6, 2026
Maker
Scandinavian Tobacco Group

Scandinavian Tobacco Group (STG) has sent retailers new price lists, which typically means that prices are going up. This time that is not what is happening: STG has made only a few minor price adjustments — a couple of items changed by just a penny — but 11 items have been removed from the General Cigar Co. price list.

The most notable removal is the Hoyo de Monterrey Epicure Selección, in all three sizes — No. 1, No. 2 and Toro. The line, which shares a very similar name with the Cuban Hoyo de Monterrey but is the separate U.S. brand, was introduced in 2020 and was highlighted by a proprietary-seed Honduran wrapper. Also removed are the Hoyo de Monterrey Maduro Governors; the El Titan de Bronze Nicaragua Corona and Robusto, whose maker — a small Miami factory — ended its distribution agreement with STG in August, though the companies say they will still work together; three Don Tomas items, the Classico Cetro #2, the Classico Corona Grande in aluminum tube and the Maduro Robusto; the Excalibur Black Robusto; and Mehari's Java Wooden.

Removal from the price list does not necessarily mean the cigars vanish from shelves: they may still be offered for sale to large catalog retailers or through STG's own retail channels.
