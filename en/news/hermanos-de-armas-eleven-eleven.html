Hermanos de Armas Ships Eleven Eleven for Veterans Day

Hermanos de Armas has begun shipping the Eleven Eleven, a 6 x 52 toro limited to 450 boxes of 22 at $16.50 per cigar, with 11 percent of gross sales going to Team SAPO, a veterans' mental-health organisation.

Published
November 10, 2026
Announced
November 7, 2026
Release
Shipping began November 7, 2026
Maker
Hermanos de Armas

Hermanos de Armas is commemorating Veterans Day with Eleven Eleven, a new limited edition cigar that has begun shipping to retailers, Halfwheel reported. The 6 x 52 toro uses a Connecticut broadleaf wrapper over a Nicaraguan binder and filler, and is produced at the La Tabacaria Cigars factory in Estelí, Nicaragua. Production is limited to 450 boxes of 22 cigars — 9,900 in total — priced at $16.50 per cigar and $363 per box.

The company will donate 11 percent of gross sales to Team SAPO, an organisation dedicated to the mental health of veterans from Tier One and Special Operations communities, offering integrative, evidence-based healing. Zack Van Ginkel, the company's co-founder and himself a veteran of the U.S. Air Force, announced the donation.

The "eleven" in the name marks the date itself: Eleven Eleven is timed to Veterans Day, November 11, and the 11 percent donation echoes it.
