Punch Nectares No. 50, Ireland's First Regional Edition, Launches in London

Ireland's first Edicion Regional revives the Nectares name discontinued in the 1980s, a Magnum 50 (160 mm x 50) in 1,400 cabinets of 50, wearing the 'Exclusivo Irlanda' band for the first time.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
February 12, 2026
Release
London launch February 5, 2026; first released in Ireland in June 2025
Maker
Habanos S.A.

The Punch Néctares No. 50, the first Habanos Regional Edition ever made for Ireland, has reached the United Kingdom, with Cigar Journal reporting that the cigars are now available in UK cigar specialists. The UK arrival was marked by a launch event on February 5 at James J. Fox in London, the retailer whose Dublin and London shops gave the cigar its name.

The Néctares No. 50 is a Magnum 50, measuring 160 mm (6 1/4 inches) with a 50 ring gauge. Its secondary band reads “Exclusivo Irlanda” — the first time that designation has appeared on a Cuban cigar. Production is limited to 1,400 numbered cabinets of 50 cigars, a total run of 70,000, priced at €65.50 (about $75.80) per cigar and €3,000 per cabinet.

The name revives a discontinued subset of the Punch brand: the original Néctares line, six numbered vitolas made exclusively for James J. Fox cigar merchants in Dublin and London, was discontinued sometime in the 1980s. The new edition was first released in Ireland in June 2025, distributed by Ampersand in collaboration with Hunters & Frankau. “Finally bringing Ireland its first Habanos Regional Edition is a moment of immense pride,” said Rob Fox, managing director of Ampersand and chair of James J. Fox, at the launch.
