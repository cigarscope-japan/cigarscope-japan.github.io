Arnold André and Joya de Nicaragua to End Distribution Partnership at Year's End

Germany's largest cigar manufacturer, Arnold André, and Nicaragua's oldest cigar maker, Joya de Nicaragua, are ending their long-standing German distribution partnership at the end of the year; no reason or successor distributor has been announced.

Published
November 10, 2026
Announced
November 9, 2026
Maker
Arnold André

Germany's largest cigar manufacturer, Arnold André GmbH, and the oldest Nicaraguan cigar manufacturer, Joya de Nicaragua, are ending their long-standing distribution partnership at the end of the year, Cigar Journal reported on November 9. Arnold André has distributed Joya's cigars in Germany under the partnership.

The brief announcement gives no reason for the split, and names no successor to handle Joya de Nicaragua's distribution in Germany from next year. Neither company has said what will follow the partnership when it ends.

The pairing joined two long-established names: one of Germany's leading cigar companies and Nicaragua's oldest cigar maker. Further details are expected if either side announces a successor arrangement.
