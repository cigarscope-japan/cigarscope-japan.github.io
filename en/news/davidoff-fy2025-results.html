Oettinger Davidoff Closes Its 150th Year With Sales Up 2.5% to CHF 545.3 Million

Oettinger Davidoff grew 2025 sales to CHF 545.3 million, up 2.5% in real terms, with Davidoff and Zino the largest growth contributors. The group kept investing in its Dominican and Honduran production sites and its global store network.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
July 1, 2026
Maker
Davidoff

Oettinger Davidoff closed its 150th anniversary year with sales turnover of CHF 545.3 million (about US$685 million) in 2025, an increase of 2.5% in real terms, despite what the company described as challenging and unstable geopolitical conditions — among them U.S. tariffs, the strong Swiss franc and changing consumer sentiment.

The growth came mainly from the company's own brands: the core Davidoff brand increased its sales by 2.4%, while Zino delivered another extraordinary year with an increase of 16.1%. Total production of handmade premium cigars in 2025 totalled 36.6 million cigars, a modest year-over-year adjustment of 4.9% to recalibrate production in response to market volatility.

The group kept investing in its "crop to shop" model and its retail network. In Villa González, Dominican Republic, a new blending center was inaugurated, while operations in Danlí, Honduras, were substantially scaled up, including the expansion of the box factory and a new fermentation complex. Seven new stores opened in 2025 and four were renovated; in 2026 the company plans to open nine new Davidoff boutiques and renovate five more. CEO Beat Hauenstein said the results reflect the Aspire727 strategy and the resilience of the group's teams around the world.
