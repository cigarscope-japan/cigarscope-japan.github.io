Punch Princesas Edición Limitada 2025 Reaches Switzerland at CHF 52

Punch's 185th-anniversary Limited Edition (135 mm x 52) went on sale at Swiss La Casa del Habano stores on February 3 and at Habanos Specialists on February 10, priced at CHF 52 per cigar.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
January 22, 2026
Release
February 3, 2026
Maker
Habanos S.A.

The Punch Princesas Edición Limitada 2025 went on sale in Switzerland in February, launched on the same dates as the Cohiba Behike BHK 58 — February 3 at La Casa del Habano franchises and February 10 at Habanos Specialists across the country — at CHF 52 (about €56 / $66) per cigar, Cigars-connect reports.

The Princesas (135 mm x 52, the Edmundo factory size) is Punch’s Limited Edition for 2025, created to mark the brand’s 185th anniversary and presented in Cyprus less than two months before the Swiss launch. It is offered in boxes of 20.
