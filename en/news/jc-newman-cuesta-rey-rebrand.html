J.C. Newman to Unveil New Cuesta-Rey Band at Big Smoke Las Vegas

J.C. Newman will debut a redesigned band for Cuesta-Rey — the brand founded in 1884 and once the official cigar of Spain's King Alfonso XIII — on November 7 at Cigar Aficionado's Big Smoke in Las Vegas.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
November 4, 2026
Release
November 7, 2026
Maker
J.C. Newman

J.C. Newman Cigar Co. has announced that it will unveil a new band design for its Cuesta-Rey line. The new bands will make their debut on November 7 at Cigar Aficionado's Big Smoke in Las Vegas, where the redesign puts the brand's crown and CR monogram at the front of a modernised treatment of its traditional marks.

Cuesta-Rey traces back to 1884, when Angel LaMadrid Cuesta and Peregrino Rey created it. It was also the official cigar of King Alfonso XIII of Spain. Today, Tabacalera A. Fuente y Cia. produces Cuesta-Rey in the Dominican Republic for J.C. Newman.
