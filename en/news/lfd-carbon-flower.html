La Flor Dominicana's Carbon Flower Ships in a Carbon-Fiber Case, Limited to 1,500 Sets

The ProjectCarbon collaboration sells a full-bodied 6 x 54 toro extra with a pigtail cap in a five-count carbon-fiber case at $200 per set, with production limited to 1,500 sets from the July 15 release.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
September 10, 2026
Release
July 15, 2026
Maker
La Flor Dominicana
Share Post on X Share on LINE Facebook

La Flor Dominicana's latest limited edition does not come in a box at all. The Carbon Flower, a collaboration with accessory maker ProjectCarbon, is sold in a five-count carbon-fiber travel case — the packaging is the point of the project. The release was announced at the 2026 PCA Convention & Trade Show in April and shipped on July 15, 2026.

The cigar itself is a 6 x 54 toro extra with a pigtail cap, rolled at Tabacalera La Flor. A Mexican San Andrés wrapper covers binder and filler tobaccos grown on the company's own La Canela farm in the Dominican Republic, and the company describes the profile as full-bodied. "Carbon is solid, it is textured, and it looms with a sort of ominous presence," said Tony Gomez of the brief behind the blend.

Pricing is $200 per set, which works out to $40 a cigar, and production is limited to 1,500 sets — 7,500 cigars in total.
