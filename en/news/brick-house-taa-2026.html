Brick House Beginnings Returns for TAA 2026 in a Five-Year-Aged Version

J.C. Newman's Ciento por Ciento is a 6 1/4 x 54 all-Nicaraguan cigar priced at $12 and limited to 8,000 cigars for TAA retailers; this version was aged for five years.

Published
November 8, 2026
Announced
October 23, 2026
Release
November 2, 2026
Maker
J.C. Newman
Share Post on X Share on LINE Facebook

J.C. Newman's Brick House Beginnings line is built around well-aged cigars, and its latest entry is reserved for members of the Tobacconists' Association of America. The Brick House Beginnings Ciento por Ciento selected for the TAA 2026 series is a five-year-aged version of the same all-Nicaraguan blend, in the same 6 1/4 x 54 size, that the company used for its TAA releases of 2019 and 2023. Ciento por Ciento — “100 percent” — refers to the all-Nicaraguan blend, capped by a habano wrapper from Jalapa.

The cigar is priced at $12, or $120 for a box of 10, and production is limited to 8,000 cigars. It was released on Nov. 2, and the company said the cigars were ready to ship, putting them on shelves from the first week of November. They are made at J.C. Newman PENSA in Estelí, Nicaragua.

An old name, an occasional series

Brick House itself dates to 1937; the name honours the only brick house in the village where J.C. Newman's great-grandfather was born. Beginnings is the occasional series the company uses for its aged-tobacco releases. The TAA counts around 80 retailers and approximately 40 manufacturers among its members, and manufacturers releasing cigars under its Exclusive Series Program agree to give the association a portion of the proceeds — a minimum of 50 cents per cigar.
