Big Smoke Las Vegas Marks 30 Years With a Weekend of Tribute to Marvin Shanken

The 30th Big Smoke Las Vegas closed at the Horseshoe Las Vegas on November 7-8, a weekend shaped by tributes to Cigar Aficionado founder Marvin R. Shanken, who died on October 29. The event returns on November 12-13, 2027.

Published
November 11, 2026
Announced
November 10, 2026
Maker
Cigar Aficionado

The 30th Big Smoke Las Vegas closed this past weekend at the Horseshoe Las Vegas, where the event was held for the second straight year, Cigar Aficionado reported. Thousands of cigar lovers from across the United States and abroad — this year including visitors from Greece, New Zealand, Scotland, England, the UAE, Mexico and Canada — gathered for two evenings of premium cigars and spirits and a Saturday of seminars.

The weekend carried a memorial weight. The industry lost Marvin R. Shanken, founder, editor and publisher of Cigar Aficionado, on October 29, and his presence ran through the show floor: J.C. Newman's grand El Reloj factory replica, set up at the centre of the hall, displayed a sign with Shanken's photograph reading "Light A Cigar For Marvin," with matching stickers handed to guests.

Among the cigarmakers on the floor were Carlos Fuente Jr., Cynthia Fuente and Carlos Fuente III, Ernesto Perez-Carrillo, Litto Gomez, Jorge and Jorge Luís Padrón, Rocky Patel, Rafael Nodal, Janny and Joseph Garcia, Pete Johnson and Drew Newman. The Big Smoke returns to Las Vegas on November 12–13, 2027.
