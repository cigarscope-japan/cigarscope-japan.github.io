ProCigar Festival 2026 Closes With a Record $618,188 Charity Auction

The 18th ProCigar Festival in Santiago ended with a gala auction totalling $618,188, a record for the Dominican association, led by a Fuente humidor that sold for $150,000 to A.J. Fernandez.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
March 2, 2026
Maker
ProCigar

The 18th ProCigar Festival closed in Santiago in February with a gala auction that raised $618,188 for charity, a record for the Dominican manufacturers' association. Nearly 1,000 guests from more than 40 countries attended the multi-day event of factory and field tours, and Dominican President Luis Abinader joined one of the evenings.

The top lot was "Les Chaînes de L'Amour," a humidor from Tabacalera Fuente, which sold for $150,000 to Nicaraguan cigarmaker A.J. Fernandez. Other headline lots included La Flor Dominicana's "Espejo de Litto" ($51,000), Tabacalera Palma's "María Victoria Palma (MVP)" ($46,000), De Los Reyes' "Horses and Tobacco" ($45,000), La Aurora's "Family Creed: Fuerte Sol" ($44,000), Casa Carrillo's "Adventure Around The World" ($42,000), General Cigar Dominicana's "CAO Selection Luxury" ($35,000), the official ProCigar humidor ($30,000) and Davidoff's "Year of The Horse Masterpiece" ($28,000). Michael Herklots led the auction.

ProCigar, founded in 1992, is the association of Dominican premium cigar manufacturers, and its festival has run since 2008. Litto Gomez of La Flor Dominicana was the association's president during the festival, which was held under the slogan "Our Soil and Soul"; his term has since ended. The auction proceeds benefit charities in the Dominican Republic.
