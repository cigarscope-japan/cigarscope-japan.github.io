Brolo Cigar Co. Begins Shipping Its First Three Blends

Texas boutique Brolo Cigar Co.'s first three blends have been released. The brand held its debut event on November 19 at Viso Cigar Lounge in McKinney, Texas, and began shipping to retailers on November 20. All three blends are 5 x 50 robustos in 20-count boxes, priced at $14.75 to $16.25, and are sold only through brick-and-mortar stores. The launch was announced in our November 9 edition; the start of shipping is the new development.

According to Cigar Dojo, Texas-based boutique Brolo Cigar Co. has released its first three blends. The brand held its debut event on November 19 at Viso Cigar Lounge in McKinney, Texas, and began shipping to retailers on November 20. Founded by husband-and-wife team Joshua and Kaitlyn Stephens, the cigars are made at a boutique factory in Nicaragua (the factory name has not been disclosed), and the founders traveled there to take part in the blending. The launch announcement was covered in our November 9 edition; the release and start of shipping are the new development.

Cigar Dojo reports that all three blends are 5 x 50 robustos in 20-count boxes. "Porchlight" wears an Ecuadorian corojo wrapper over Nicaraguan and Dominican leaf, a medium-bodied blend at $14.75. "Confessional" is a double habano with an Ecuadorian habano rosado wrapper, described with baking spice, dark fruit, cherry and coffee notes, also $14.75. "Back Booth" uses a Mexican San Andrés maduro wrapper, with darker flavors of wood, walnut, cedar and raisin, at $16.25.

As Cigar Dojo reports, sales are through brick-and-mortar stores only, with no direct sales. The brand flies the banner "Patina Over Polish" and runs a Discord community and virtual herfs, making it a much-talked-about new label.
