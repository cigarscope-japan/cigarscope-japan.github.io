ADVentura Marks Its 10th Anniversary with First Decade, Rolled with First-Harvest Leaf from Its Own Farm

ADV (ADVentura Cigars, ADV & McKay), a Swiss-born boutique made in the Dominican Republic, has unveiled "First Decade," a cigar marking its 10th anniversary. It pairs an Ecuadorian Habano Escuro wrapper with binder and filler from "Aromatica," a proprietary variety developed on the company's own farm — first-harvest leaf aged five years. It comes in a single size, a 6 x 52 toro. Production is limited to 2,000 boxes of 10 (20,000 cigars), each with a removable tray. It is scheduled for release in January 2027, with the price still to be announced.

According to CigarsLover, ADV (ADVentura Cigars, ADV & McKay), a Swiss-born boutique made in the Dominican Republic, has unveiled "First Decade," a cigar marking its 10th anniversary. It pairs an Ecuadorian Habano Escuro wrapper with binder and filler from "Aromatica," a proprietary variety developed on the company's own farm — first-harvest leaf that was set aside and aged for five years, a return to the company's roots, in its words. It comes in a single size, a 6 x 52 toro.

Production is limited to 2,000 boxes of 10 (20,000 cigars), each box fitted with a removable tray. The cigar is scheduled for release in January 2027, with the price still to be announced.

ADVentura "First Decade": Ecuadorian Habano Escuro wrapper / "Aromatica," a proprietary variety from the company's own farm (binder and filler, first-harvest leaf) / Toro 6 x 52, one size only / 2,000 boxes of 10 (20,000 cigars), each with a removable tray / Scheduled for January 2027, price to be announced
