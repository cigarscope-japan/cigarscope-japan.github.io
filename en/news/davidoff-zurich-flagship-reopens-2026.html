According to Cigar Coop, Davidoff has formally reopened its "Davidoff of Geneva since 1911" flagship store in Zurich, Switzerland, after a renovation. The ribbon-cutting ceremony took place on November 19, following seven weeks of work, on the same day as the lighting of Zurich's "Lucy" Christmas illumination. The store stands in the Bahnhofplatz district and traces its history to "Zigarren Dürr," founded in 1903. The centrepiece of the redesign is a glass-walled walk-in humidor with a tobacco-leaf display, described as an embodiment of Davidoff's "crop to shop" philosophy. Oettinger Davidoff CEO Beat Hauenstein said the new design marks the start of a new chapter, expressing the hope that seasoned aficionados and curious newcomers alike will find inspiration, connection and enjoyment there.
