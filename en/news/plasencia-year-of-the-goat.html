Plasencia Unveils El Año de la Cabra, Its Seventh Zodiac Cigar

The seventh Plasencia zodiac release is a single 6 1/4 x 60 perfecto rolled in Honduras from mostly Honduran leaf, limited to 6,000 boxes of ten at $600, shipping at the end of November.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
November 3, 2026
Release
End of November 2026 (shipping expected)
Maker
Plasencia Cigars

Plasencia Cigars has unveiled El Año de la Cabra — Year of the Goat — the company's seventh annual release styled on the animals of the Chinese zodiac, and it is expected to ship at the end of November. "The goat represents a quiet but powerful strength, one that comes from patience, balance and an instinctive connection to nature," said fifth-generation owner Néstor Andrés Plasencia.

The cigar comes in a single size: a perfecto that tapers at both ends, 6 1/4 inches long by 60 ring gauge at its fattest point. It is rolled at Plasencia's factory in Honduras and consists predominantly of Honduran tobacco — a wrapper grown in Azacualpa in the north of the country, binder and filler from the Jamastrán Valley, and some Nicaraguan leaf from Estelí in the filler — all of it grown by the Plasencias. The company rates the blend medium-to-full in strength.

El Año de la Cabra is packaged in red lacquered, octagonal boxes with gold detailing and a swirling pattern that evokes a ram's horn; inspired by the hóngbāo red envelope, the set includes red cardboard tubes in which the cigars can be handed out as gifts. Production is limited to 6,000 boxes of ten worldwide, with 3,500 allocated to the U.S. market, and the price is $600 a box, or $60 per cigar.
