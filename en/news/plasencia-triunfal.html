Plasencia Triunfal Pairs Honduran and Nicaraguan Organic Leaf for the 2026 World Cup

Plasencia's second straight World Cup cigar is a 6 1/4 x 54 gran toro limited to 5,000 numbered boxes at $59.99 — the company's first cigar to combine certified organic tobacco from both Honduras and Nicaragua.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
May 12, 2026
Release
May 2026 (reported arriving at stores)
Maker
Plasencia Cigars

For the second World Cup in a row, Plasencia has built a cigar around the tournament. Triunfal follows the Ehtëfal of 2022 and was unveiled at the PCA 2026 trade show; it is now reaching stores. The company says it is the first cigar it has released combining certified organic tobacco from both Honduras and Nicaragua.

The single size is a 6 1/4 x 54 gran toro. The wrapper comes from Finca La Tradición in Honduras's Jamastrán Valley and the binder from Finca San Agustín in Olancho, with fillers from La Tradición and from Jalapa, Nicaragua. Every cigar was rolled by a single pair of workers at the Tabacos de Oriente factory in Danlí, Honduras. Production is limited to 5,000 numbered boxes of ten, at $59.99 per cigar and $599 per box.

The packaging carries its own firsts: a green, stadium-pitch design whose lid is textured like the veins of a tobacco leaf, and the debut of Plasencia's new logo, shown in gold on both box and band. The company's marketing describes the flavour in terms of toffee, cedar, creaminess, toasted almonds, molasses and earth. "Triunfal represents much more than a cigar," said José Luis Plasencia, a fifth-generation member of the family. "It reflects unity, resilience, and celebration."
