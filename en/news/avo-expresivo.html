AVO Expresivo Launches as the Brand's First Honduran-Made Line

Davidoff's AVO brand shipped Expresivo on June 25, its first line made in Honduras — at the Diadema factory best known for Camacho — and its first new regular production line since Syncro Caribe in 2021.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
June 25, 2026
Release
June 25, 2026
Maker
Davidoff

June 25 saw the launch of the AVO Expresivo, a new chapter for Davidoff's AVO brand. For the first time in the brand's history, an AVO line is made in Honduras: Expresivo is produced at Diadema Cigars de Honduras S.A., Davidoff's Honduran factory best known as the home of Camacho, while every other AVO is made in the Dominican Republic. Davidoff describes the cigar as stronger than a typical AVO.

The blend uses an Ecuadorian wrapper over a Honduran binder, with fillers from Honduras and from Jalapa, Nicaragua. It is the first new regular production AVO line since the Syncro Caribe launched in 2021. The packaging is also new, and Davidoff has said the wider AVO brand will undergo a makeover in the coming years, with many of the elements found on the Expresivo packaging coming to other lines.

Expresivo is offered in three sizes, all in boxes of 20: a Robusto (5 x 52, $16), a Toro (6 x 54, $17.80) and a Churchill (7 x 48, $17.30). The following day, June 26, Davidoff also launched the Zino Honduras, another new line made at the same Diadema factory.
