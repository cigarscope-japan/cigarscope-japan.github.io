Forged Announces Weller by Cohiba 2026, With a Binder Aged in Weller C.Y.P.B. Barrels

Forged Cigar Company has informed retailers of the Weller by Cohiba 2026, the sixth edition of the Buffalo Trace collaboration — a 6 x 50 toro at $34.99 whose USA broadleaf binder was aged in Weller C.Y.P.B. barrels, sold in tubes in boxes of 10 at $349.90.

Announced to retailers November 11, 2026

Forged Cigar Company has informed retailers of the Weller by Cohiba 2026, the sixth consecutive edition of Cohiba's collaboration with Buffalo Trace's Weller bourbon. This year's distinguishing feature is the binder: the USA broadleaf binder was aged in barrels that held Weller C.Y.P.B. — Craft Your Perfect Bourbon — the first time those barrels have been used for the line. Earlier editions used Weller 12 Year barrels (2022 and 2023), Full Proof barrels (2024) and Antique 107 barrels (2025).

The blend pairs an Ecuadorian Sumatra wrapper with Honduran, Nicaraguan and Dominican fillers, and is made at General Cigar Dominicana in the Dominican Republic. There is a single size, a 6 x 50 toro, each cigar packaged in a white-and-gold tube styled after the C.Y.P.B. packaging.

The tubes come in boxes of 10. Pricing is $34.99 per cigar (about ¥5,580) and $349.90 per box (about ¥55,800).
