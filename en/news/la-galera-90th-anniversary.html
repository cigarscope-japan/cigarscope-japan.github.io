La Galera Marks 90 Years With Its First Dominican Puro and a Homegrown Wrapper

For the 90th anniversary of the La Palma registration, Tabacalera Palma releases its first Dominican puro and its first line using wrapper it grew itself, in the box-pressed Lector and the new Nanán perfecto, released Aug. 7 as regular production.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
August 10, 2026
Release
August 7, 2026
Maker
La Galera (Tabacalera Palma)

Tabacalera Palma is marking 90 years since its "La Palma" registration with the La Galera 90th Anniversary, a release that gives the Dominican company two firsts at once: its first Dominican puro, and the first line to use wrapper leaf the company has grown itself. The operation was founded in 1925 by José Arnaldo Blanco II and registered as La Palma in 1936, the date the company has long used as its founding year; it is run today by José Arnaldo Blanco III.

Every leaf comes from the company's own farms. The wrapper is corojo from the Jacagua farm, the binder piloto cubano from Gurabo, and the fillers combine leaves from Gurabo, Jacagua and La Canela. "Growing wrapper is hard," Blanco said of the milestone, noting how difficult wrapper cultivation has proven in the Dominican Republic.

Two box-pressed sizes are offered as regular production: the Lector (5 5/8 x 54, $18.06) and the Nanán (6 3/4 x 44/58, $27.22), an all-new perfecto developed for this release that pairs a sharp four-sided press with a rounded torpedo head. The cigars began shipping on Aug. 7, 2026.
