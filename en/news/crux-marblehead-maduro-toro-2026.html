Crux Marblehead Maduro Toro Scores 88 — "Buy Multiples" at Under $8

Cigar Coop has reviewed the Toro of Crux's new 2026 line, Marblehead Maduro, scoring it 88 points with a "Buy Multiples" verdict. The 6 x 50 toro uses a Mexican San Andrés maduro wrapper with Nicaraguan and Indonesian binder and filler, rolled at Tabacalera Pages in Nicaragua. Its tapered head imitates the Cuban "109" (bullet cap), a concept the company has carried since its founding in 2014. The Natural (Habano wrapper) launched early in the year; the Maduro was unveiled at PCA 2026 and began shipping June 1. Flavors center on coffee, chocolate, oak and pepper, with strength and body building from medium to medium-full. Slightly difficult combustion, requiring frequent touch-ups, was the main deduction. It is $7.99 per cigar, in two sizes — Toro 6 x 50 and Gordo 6 x 60 — in a distinctive clear 20-count PVC bundle (four 5-packs) and a 60-count display tray. The line was also named one of The Cigar Authority's seven "2026 Cigar of the Year" contenders, announced November 21.
