Tatuaje's Creature Is the Monster Series' 101st Release, Now in Full Size

The Creature has become the 14th full-size Monster: a 6 7/8 x 52 all-Nicaraguan perfecto made at My Father Cigars, priced at $19.54 after the 1954 film that inspired it and limited to 7,000 boxes of 10. It began shipping in mid-June, well ahead of Halloween.

Maker
Tatuaje Cigars

Earlier this year, Tatuaje deviated from its recent pattern. In April, at the 2026 PCA Convention & Trade Show, it showed off the Creature from Tatuaje, a 6 7/8 x 52 perfecto with an all-Nicaraguan blend, made at My Father Cigars S.A. in Estelí. Unlike the recent Monster Series Redux cigars, this is a genuinely new full-size Monster — the 101st release in the Monster Series, and the full-size version of two previous Creature releases.

The blend first appeared in smaller form in samplers: the Monster Mash Creature of September 2021 and the Monster Smash Creature of August 2024. In keeping with the series' movie-monster names, the Creature takes its name from "Creature from the Black Lagoon", the 1954 film about an amphibious humanoid found in the Amazon rainforest — and its $19.54 price nods to that year. In a break with convention, the name drops the "The" that has prefaced Monster releases since the start, and the cigars come in 10-count boxes rather than the coffin-style 13-count boxes of earlier releases. Production is limited to 7,000 boxes of 10 cigars.

The Creature shipped to stores in the middle of June 2026, well ahead of the Halloween holiday the line has been associated with since its 2008 debut. Reviewing the cigar for halfwheel, Patrick Lagreid scored it 84, finding earth and cream early on but judging it a slimmer expression of what Tatuaje and My Father can do than the earlier sampler versions.
