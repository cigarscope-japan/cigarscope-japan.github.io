Davidoff Ends 34 Years as a ProCigar Member

A founding member through its Tabadom Holding operation, Davidoff has left the Dominican manufacturers' association to concentrate resources on global priorities and regulatory challenges across its 130 markets, leaving ProCigar with 11 full members.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
September 4, 2026
Maker
Davidoff
Share Post on X Share on LINE Facebook

Davidoff has ended its membership in ProCigar, the Dominican Republic's association of premium cigar manufacturers, closing a 34-year affiliation. The company's Dominican operation, Tabadom Holding, was a founding member of the organisation, which leaves ProCigar with 11 full members.

The company said the decision reflects a shift toward focusing resources on global priorities and regulatory challenges across its 130 markets, in a statement reported by Halfwheel. Davidoff added that it will continue working directly with Dominican authorities to support the sustainable growth of the cigar industry.

Neither Davidoff nor ProCigar identified a specific event behind the decision. The departure had been foreshadowed in February, when the company's participation in the 2026 ProCigar Festival was already reduced — a contrast with the festival auction the same month, where a Davidoff humidor still ranked among the top lots.
