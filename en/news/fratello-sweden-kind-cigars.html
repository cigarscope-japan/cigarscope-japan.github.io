Fratello Cigars Adds Distribution in Sweden Through Kind Cigars

Fratello has signed Kind Cigars — founded in 2011 by Martin Nelson — to distribute its cigars in Sweden, a deal that began with a meeting at InterTabac and extends a network of some thirty markets.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
November 5, 2026
Maker
Fratello Cigars

Fratello Cigars has entered into a distribution agreement with Kind Cigars that will bring its cigars to Sweden, the company has announced. Kind Cigars was founded in 2011 by Martin Nelson and currently distributes a number of cigar companies in the Swedish market.

"Sweden has one of the most knowledgeable and passionate cigar audiences in Europe," said Omar de Frias, owner and founder of Fratello Cigars, in a press release. "From the moment we met at Intertabac, it was clear we shared the similar values: quality, honesty, and a genuine love for cigars." Kind Cigars' Nelson said the company had followed the brand's growth across Europe for some time and was proud to welcome it to Sweden.

Sweden joins a distribution footprint that already spans some thirty markets and territories, including the Netherlands, Spain, Thailand, Switzerland, Germany and the United Kingdom — another step in the American brand's steady expansion across Europe.
