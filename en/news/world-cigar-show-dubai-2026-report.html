World Cigar Show Dubai Opens Its Second Edition, With Fuente and Padrón Among First-Time Exhibitors

The second World Cigar Show, presented in partnership with the PCA, runs November 10-11 at the Dubai World Trade Centre. Arturo Fuente, Casa Carrillo, Padrón and Rocky Patel exhibited for the first time, and a conference programme led by a keynote from the Dominican Republic's ambassador to the UAE anchors the final day today.

Published
November 11, 2026
Announced
November 10, 2026
Maker
World Cigar Show

The second edition of World Cigar Show, presented in partnership with the Premium Cigar Association (PCA), runs November 10–11 at the Dubai World Trade Centre, and the first day pointed to a show gaining momentum in the Middle East's fast-growing premium cigar market. Today, November 11, is the final day.

Arturo Fuente, Casa Carrillo, Padrón and Rocky Patel exhibited for the first time, showcased together by Baqer Mohebi Enterprises at Stand C240, while the PCA met members and industry professionals at Stand C220. The wider floor included Capitol, VegaFina, God of Fire, Blackbird Cigars, Principle Cigars, JM Tobacco, Davtian Premium Cigars, Garo Cigars, Gerard, DBA Thai Cigars, La Flor de Portuondo, DH Signature, Clair De Brume, Puros Artesanos Import, SikarInfuse and The Leaf Master, alongside suppliers of machinery, packaging, humidors and accessories. Vintage Cigars sponsored a smoking lounge, and The Leaf Master hosted a cigar terrace.

The conference programme looks at the premium cigar business through heritage, luxury, hospitality and international commerce, including consumer behaviour in Asia, the hospitality-driven market in the Middle East and opportunities in Africa. H.E. Renso Herrera Franco, Ambassador of the Dominican Republic to the UAE, delivers a keynote, with welcome remarks from PCA CEO Joshua Habursky and participation by Ricardo Carioni, the PCA's Director of International Affairs and Education. A World Cigar Show 2026 podcast is also being recorded on site, with Carlito Fuente Jr. among the guests.

Four separately ticketed social events frame the visit: Sea Smoke, an evening dhow cruise on Dubai Canal on November 10; Desert Smoke, a desert barbecue evening on November 11; and on November 12, a luncheon with the PCA's CEO and a Private Yacht Cigar Night at Dubai Harbour.
