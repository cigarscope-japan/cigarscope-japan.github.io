Selected Tobacco Opens Five-Storey Madrid Headquarters, Reviving Cigar Making in the Capital

Nelson Alfonso's Selected Tobacco celebrated the grand opening of its five-storey Madrid facility on October 10-11 — the first premium cigar production site in the city in more than half a century, with a gala for some 250 to 300 guests.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
November 1, 2026
Maker
Selected Tobacco

Selected Tobacco, the company behind Atabey and Byron, has opened a five-storey headquarters and production facility in Madrid, celebrating with a grand opening weekend on October 10-11. The roughly 3,000-square-metre building stands near Madrid-Barajas Airport in the northeast of the city. According to the company, it is the first premium cigar production facility to open in Madrid in more than 50 years; the October 11 gala drew between 250 and 300 guests.

The ground floor combines reception, the warehouse and an art gallery showing founder Nelson Alfonso's paintings. Design and packaging sit on the second floor; the third holds the rolling room — the galera — and aging rooms; the fourth adds further aging rooms, executive offices and a showroom; and the fifth is a private members' lounge with a terrace. Behind the building are fermentation areas and a hydroponic tobacco laboratory where Alfonso works with experimental seed.

For now, the actual rolling remains outsourced: aged tobacco is shipped to Tabacos de Costa Rica, and the finished cigars return to Madrid for further aging. But the third-floor galera has begun work with six pairs of rollers, and the company's stated goal is to roll 500,000 cigars a year in Spain.
