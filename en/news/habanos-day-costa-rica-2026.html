Fifth Habanos Day Draws More Than 100 Guests to Costa Rica

Costa Rica hosted the fifth Habanos Day on May 16, organised by distributor Cruz Canela, with tastings centred on three anniversary brands — Cohiba at 60 and Cuaba and Vegueros at 30.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
June 3, 2026
Maker
Habanos S.A.

Costa Rica hosted the fifth edition of Habanos Day on May 16, 2026, an event organised by Cruz Canela, the exclusive distributor of Habanos S.A. for Costa Rica and Nicaragua. More than 100 guests — sommeliers, mixologists and Habanos enthusiasts from different countries — took part, Habanos S.A. reported.

The programme began on May 13 at La Casa del Habano in San José, with a pairing of Romeo y Julieta Wide Churchills and the Montecristo Regata with Ron Eminente cocktails. The main event followed on May 16 at Chateau 1525, in the province of Heredia, where the Habanos were shown alongside displays of cars, art, jewellery and watches.

The tastings centred on brands celebrating anniversaries this year: Cohiba, in its 60th anniversary, was present with the Siglo I and Siglo III; Cuaba, at 30, with its Exclusivos; and Vegueros, also at 30, with the Centrofinos. A twelve-course menu fused Costa Rican and Cuban cuisine.
