Aladino 250th Ships June 16: 1,000 Boxes of 13 for America's 250th Anniversary

JRE Tobacco's 6 x 52 toro for the 250th anniversary of America's founding uses only company-grown tobacco at $15, limited to 1,000 boxes of 13 — a count chosen for the 13 original colonies.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
June 15, 2026
Release
June 16, 2026
Maker
JRE Tobacco (Aladino)

On June 16, JRE Tobacco Co. shipped the cigar it made for the 250th anniversary of the founding of America: the Aladino 250th. The 6 x 52 toro is made entirely of tobacco grown by the company itself, though JRE is declining to say which tobaccos are used. The Eiroa family, which owns JRE, is one of Honduras's most prominent tobacco growers.

Each cigar carries an MSRP of $15, and production is limited to 1,000 boxes of 13 cigars — the count was chosen to honor the 13 original colonies.

The cigar had a different name at first. When the company announced it in early March, it was called the Aladino Declaration; the name has since been changed over a trademark concern, though the original Declaration name can still be seen on early bands before it was replaced by 250th.
