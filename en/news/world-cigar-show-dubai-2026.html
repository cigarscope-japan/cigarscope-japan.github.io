Arturo Fuente, Casa Carrillo, Padrón and Rocky Patel Join World Cigar Show Dubai Lineup

Four major brands exhibit for the first time at the PCA-partnered World Cigar Show at the Dubai World Trade Centre on November 10–11, showcased by Baqer Mohebi Enterprises.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
October 27, 2026
Maker
World Cigar Show

The World Cigar Show returns to the Dubai World Trade Centre on November 10–11, 2026, presented in partnership with the Premium Cigar Association (PCA) — and this year Arturo Fuente, Casa Carrillo, Padrón Cigars and Rocky Patel join the lineup as first-time exhibitors, adding momentum to the young event. Registration is complimentary for professionals working in the sector.

The four brands will be showcased by Baqer Mohebi Enterprises at Stand C240, within a broader exhibitor list that spans cigar brands alongside machinery, packaging, humidor and accessory suppliers; PCA itself will be at Stand C220. The conference programme will examine the premium cigar business through heritage, luxury, hospitality and international commerce, with sessions on consumer behaviour in Asia, the Middle East's hospitality-driven cigar market and opportunities in Africa. H.E. Renso Herrera Franco, Ambassador of the Dominican Republic to the UAE, will deliver a keynote, with welcome remarks from PCA CEO Joshua Habursky.

Beyond the show floor, PCA and its partners are offering four separately ticketed social experiences across November 10–12 — a dhow cruise on Dubai Canal, a desert evening, a luncheon with the PCA's CEO and a private yacht night at Dubai Harbour — underlining the Gulf's growing place in the geography of the premium cigar trade.
