Trinidad Corcel, the Brand's First Chinese New Year Release, Debuts in Hong Kong

The 55 x 135 mm Patricios, Trinidad's first special release honouring the Lunar New Year, made its world debut on January 29 in Hong Kong, in individually numbered cases of 18 with NFC authentication.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
January 29, 2026
Release
January 29, 2026
Maker
Habanos S.A.

Habanos, S.A. has presented the Trinidad Corcel, the first special release from the Trinidad brand created in honour of the Chinese Lunar New Year, drawing inspiration from the strength and indomitable spirit embodied by the horse. The global launch took place on January 29 at the Emerald & Pearl Pavilion of the Hopewell Hotel in Hong Kong, organised by Pacific Cigar Corporation, the official Habanos distributor for Asia-Pacific, and brought together over 180 enthusiasts from around the world.

The Corcel carries the factory name Patricios and measures 55 ring gauge x 135 mm, a parejo with pigtail made with leaves from the Vuelta Abajo zone of Pinar del Río. Habanos describes it as a balanced, medium-strength smoke of about an hour and a half. It is presented in elegant, individually numbered cases of 18 cigars, incorporating NFC technology in the case to provide product information and guarantee authenticity.

“It is a great honour to welcome you to the global launch of our latest addition to the Trinidad family: Trinidad Corcel, a celebration commemorating the Year of the Horse,” said Dag Holmboe, CEO of Pacific Cigar Corporation, at the launch. Habanos said the Hong Kong debut marks the beginning of the vitola's expansion into other markets in the coming months.
