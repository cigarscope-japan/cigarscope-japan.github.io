Cohiba Talisman Joins the Linea Clasica as a Regular Release

First sold as a 2017 Limited Edition, the 54 x 154 mm Talisman becomes the ninth regular vitola of the Linea Clasica; its London launch on Oct. 7 opened the Cohiba 60th anniversary year.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
October 7, 2026
Release
October 7, 2026
Maker
Habanos S.A.

Habanos S.A. has officially launched the “Year of Cohiba” with the global unveiling of the Cohiba Talismán in London — the latest addition to the brand's Línea Clásica and the first major milestone in the celebrations of Cohiba's 60th anniversary. Originally introduced in 2017 as a Limited Edition, the Talismán now joins the regular portfolio as the ninth vitola in the Línea Clásica, the first line created for Cohiba.

The Talismán is presented in the Sublimes No. 1 format, 54 ring gauge x 154 mm, a parejo with pigtail offering a medium-to-full-bodied smoke of about 60 to 90 minutes. It comes in a 10-count Boîte Nature box with refreshed packaging — to be rolled out progressively across the rest of the Línea Clásica — including NFC technology in the label inside the box for authenticity verification, and a foot band displaying the vitola's name. A gift pack of three is exclusive to the UK, at a recommended price of £600 per pack (about $790).

The global launch took place on Wednesday, October 7 in London before around 400 guests, including the UK distributor Hunters & Frankau. “Talismán is not only the first launch of this Cohiba's 60th anniversary,” said José María López Inchaurbe, Vice-President of Development at Habanos S.A. “It also marks the beginning of the Año Cohiba in terms of new releases. And believe me, Talismán is just the beginning of everything is yet to come.”
