Saint Luis Rey Eiroa 30th Anniversary Goes Maduro, Shipping to TAA Retailers First

Altadis U.S.A. and Christian Eiroa add a darker Maduro expression to their 30th Anniversary line, in four sizes at $12 to $13.50, made at the C.L.E. factory in Danli and sold only through brick-and-mortar shops.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
November 5, 2026
Release
Mid-November 2026 (TAA member retailers first)
Maker
Altadis U.S.A.

The commemorative Saint Luis Rey line from Altadis U.S.A. and Christian Eiroa of C.L.E. Cigar Co. now has a darker, bolder counterpart. Earlier this year the two teamed up to release the Saint Luis Rey Eiroa 30th Anniversary, a modern recreation of the first Honduran version of Saint Luis Rey, which Eiroa blended in 1996. The new Maduro version, introduced at the Tobacconists' Association of America meeting in late October, is designed as a more intense expression of the same concept.

The Maduro is rolled with an Ecuadorian Habano maduro wrapper, a Honduran Corojo binder and fillers of Colombian Pinareño and Honduran Corojo. It comes in four sizes — Rothchilde (5 x 50), Torpedo (6 x 52), Toro (6 x 54) and Titan (6 x 60) — in 25-count wooden boxes, priced from $12 to $13.50 per cigar. Production is at the C.L.E. factory in Danlí, Honduras.

"The Saint Luis Rey Eiroa 30th Anniversary Maduro represents the continuation of a chapter we began in 1996 but never had the opportunity to bring to market," Eiroa said. The release is exclusive to brick-and-mortar retailers: TAA member shops receive it first, with shipments expected in November, and other retailers should be supplied by the PCA trade show in March 2027.
