Por Larrañaga 35 Aniversario Marks 5th Avenue's Anniversary in Germany

The German regional edition (194 mm x 49) went on sale August 31 at EUR 35 per cigar and EUR 1,750 per box of 50, limited to 100,000 cigars, for distributor 5th Avenue's 35th anniversary.

Published

The Por Larrañaga 35 Aniversario Edición Regional Alemania went on sale in Germany on August 31, offered exclusively at La Casa del Habano and Habanos Specialist stores in the country, Halfwheel reports. The cigar (7 5/8 inches / 194 mm x 49) was commissioned by 5th Avenue Products Trading GmbH, the Habanos distributor for Germany, Austria and Poland, to celebrate the company’s 35th anniversary.

It is priced at €35 (about $41) per cigar and €1,750 (about $2,030) per box of 50, with production limited to 100,000 cigars. The vitola has appeared in the Regional Edition programme before — for India’s Punch Platino and Spain’s Sancho Panza Quijote — but this is the first time a German regional edition has used it, and the 35 Aniversario is the fourth Por Larrañaga regional edition the distributor has commissioned.
