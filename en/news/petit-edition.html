Selected Tobacco's Petit Edition Packs Five Tubed Cigars and Two New Vitolas

The $164 five-cigar set adds the Bandolero Premium Intrépidos and Nelson Nobles, with shipping to retailers scheduled to begin in mid-November.

Published
November 8, 2026
Announced
November 3, 2026
Release
Mid-November 2026 (shipping to retailers begins)
Maker
Selected Tobacco

For the second month in a row, Selected Tobacco has announced a sampler built around two new cigars. The Petit Edition Set packages five cigars in individual aluminium tubes, each from a different Selected Tobacco brand, and two of them are new vitolas for their lines.

The new arrivals are the Bandolero Premium Intrépidos (4 x 50) and the Nelson Nobles (4 x 40). They are joined by three existing releases: the Atabey Idolos (4 x 55), the Byron Petit Poemas (4 x 50) and the Alfonso Añejo No. 1 (4 x 50).

Rolled in Costa Rica, aged in Spain

After rolling in Costa Rica, the cigars are sent to Spain, where they spend four to five years in specialised aging rooms lined with five varieties of cedar — the measured aging that Selected Tobacco treats as part of the making itself. “For Nelson Alfonso, aging is an essential part of the work itself; the final character of a cigar develops long after it leaves the rolling table,” the company's press release reads.

Each Petit Edition Set has an MSRP of $164, and the sets are scheduled to begin shipping to retailers in mid-November. The announcement follows last month's Robusto Edition Set, which likewise introduced two new vitolas.
