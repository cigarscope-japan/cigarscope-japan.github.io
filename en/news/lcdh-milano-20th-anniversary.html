La Casa del Habano Milano Celebrates 20 Years at the Portrait Milano

Brothers Luca and Simone Borla marked two decades of La Casa del Habano Milano with an anniversary evening at the Portrait Milano, unveiling a DeArt commemorative humidor limited to 50 pieces.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
May 9, 2026
Maker
Habanos S.A.

La Casa del Habano Milano has celebrated its twentieth anniversary with an evening at the Portrait Milano, where brothers Luca and Simone Borla hosted thirty special guests. The celebration was held on April 23; Habanos S.A. published its report on May 9. Among the guests were Habanos vice presidents José María López Inchaurbe and Borja Casanova, and the leadership of Diadema S.p.A., the exclusive Habanos distributor for Italy.

The evening opened with the Ramón Allones Noellas Edición Regional Italia 2020, presented in glass jars to commemorate Diadema’s own twentieth anniversary. The Montecristo Herederos was savoured during the night, and the La Escepción Selectos Reales Edición Regional Italia 2025 — a campanas of 52 ring gauge by 140 mm, in cabinets of 50 and carrying a “25 Aniversario Diadema” inscription — was presented.

The anniversary also saw the unveiling of the commemorative La Casa del Habano Milano humidor, crafted by DeArt: a unique piece, numbered and limited to 50 examples, housing 20 Bolívar New Gold Medal cigars. The finale paired a Trinidad 2006 17-year-old Flag Series rum, bottled exclusively for the store in 120 bottles, with Macallan whiskies from 1958 and 1963.
