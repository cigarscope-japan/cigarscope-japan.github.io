Bolívar Evento 55, France's 2025 Regional Edition, Reaches Tobacconists at €35

The Bolívar Evento 55 Edición Regional France 2025 — a 140 mm x 52 genios, a first for the brand — is on sale in France in 4,000 cabinets of 50 at €35 a cigar.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
July 7, 2026
Release
July 7, 2026
Maker
Habanos S.A.

The Bolívar Evento 55 Edición Regional France 2025, announced the previous month by distributor Coprova, is now available at French tobacconists, Cigars-connect reported on July 7, 2026.

The cigar is a gran robusto measuring 140 mm in length with a 52 ring gauge — a size known in Cuban factories as genios. True to the regional concept, the size is a first for the Bolívar brand; it is identical to that of the Cohiba Maduro 5 Genios. Production is limited to 4,000 cabinets of 50 cigars, a total of 200,000 cigars, and the Evento 55 retails at €35 per cigar (about $41).

Coprova also announced the simultaneous launch of a limited-edition Cohiba Wide Short box: 50 of the cigarillos in a box priced at €340, with 21,000 numbered boxes available worldwide.
