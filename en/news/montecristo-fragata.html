Montecristo Fragata Debuts as a Duty-Free and Travel Retail Exclusive

Unveiled at TFWA in Cannes on Sept. 29, the new Duke-format vitola (54 x 140 mm) comes only in a 20-cigar travel humidor priced at $1,280, exclusively for duty-free and travel retail channels.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
September 29, 2026
Release
Unveiled September 29, 2026, at TFWA Cannes; on-sale date not announced
Maker
Habanos S.A.

Habanos S.A. unveiled the Montecristo Fragata at the TFWA World Exhibition & Conference in Cannes on September 29 — a travel humidor created exclusively for the duty-free and travel retail channels. The humidor contains 20 Fragata cigars, a brand-new vitola in the Duke format, 54 ring gauge x 140 mm, with the classic Montecristo blend at medium to full strength, made totally handmade with long filler from the Vuelta Abajo zone of Pinar del Río. Each cigar carries a special foot band bearing the name of the vitola.

The recommended retail price is $64 (€55) per cigar and $1,280 (€1,100) per humidor. The name Fragata — frigate — echoes the ships that figure in The Count of Monte Cristo, the Alexandre Dumas novel from which the brand, founded in Havana in 1935 and recently celebrating its 90th anniversary, takes its name. Habanos S.A. noted it has now marketed exclusive products for the duty-free and travel retail channels for 20 years, with nearly 20 such offerings.
