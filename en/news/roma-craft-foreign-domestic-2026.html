RoMa Craft Sends Foreign & Domestic Back to Stores in a Second, Larger Run

The second release of RoMa Craft Tobac's CRAFT Maquette Foreign & Domestic, a 4 x 46 box-pressed petite corona, is heading to stores at $10 per cigar, with production up from 500 bundles to 1,200.

Published
November 10, 2026
Announced
November 6, 2026
Release
Released November 6, 2026
Maker
RoMa Craft Tobac

The sophomore release of RoMa Craft Tobac's CRAFT Maquette Foreign & Domestic is heading to stores, Halfwheel reported. The 4 x 46 box-pressed petite corona returns at the same $10 per cigar, in the same craft-paper bundles of 10, but the production run has grown from 500 bundles to 1,200 — 12,000 cigars in total. It is the first time a CRAFT Maquette cigar has been released twice.

The blend, undisclosed at the 2025 debut, is now public: an Ecuadorian Sumatra-hybrid wrapper over an Indonesian Besuki binder, with corojo and criollo fillers from the Jamastrán Valley in Honduras and tobacco from Condega, Nicaragua. Co-founder Skip Martin says the blend is nearly identical to two other RoMa Craft releases — the Riverside 15th Anniversary, a store exclusive, and the upcoming CroMagnon Visigoth, whose launch has slipped from late 2026 to next year. The cigars are made at Fábrica de Tabacos Nica Sueño S.A. in Estelí.

The name reflects the company's split geography: Martin lives in Nicaragua, where the cigars are made, while co-founder Michael Rosales lives in Austin, Texas, where the company is headquartered. It also echoes the oath taken by members of the military and other government officials to defend the U.S. Constitution "against all enemies, foreign and domestic." The cigars were released on November 6, 2026.
