La Flor de Cano Mágicos Returns to Spain as Aged Stock Under 'Fuera de Serie'

Distributor Tabacalera S.L.U. has re-released aged stock of Spain's 2018 Regional Edition La Flor de Cano Mágicos under its 'Fuera de Serie' aging program, at €20 a cigar against €8 at its 2020 debut.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
May 21, 2026
Release
May 2026 (aged-stock re-release reported on May 21, 2026)
Maker
Habanos S.A.

Spain’s La Flor de Cano Mágicos is back on sale — not as a new cigar, but as aged stock. Cigar Journal reported on May 21 that the discontinued Regional Edition has returned to the Spanish market under the “Fuera de Serie” concept, and Cigar Aficionado’s same-day report sets out the details: distributor Tabacalera S.L.U. has gone into its inventory of aged stock and re-released a limited quantity of the cigars under its “Fuera de Serie” (“out of series”) aging program.

The Mágicos measures 4 1/2 inches by 52 ring gauge (115 mm x 52) and shares its dimensions with the Cohiba Maduro 5 cigar of the same name. It was officially designated Spain’s 2018 Regional Edition but was not released until March 2020, and it marked the first and only time the La Flor de Cano brand has appeared in Spain as a Regional Edition. Each 10-count box of the re-release bears a “Fuera de Serie” sticker on the outer lid, indicating the aging concept.

With extra age comes extra cost: the Mágicos debuted at a suggested retail price of €8 (about $9 in 2020), while the re-release is priced at €20 apiece (around $23), an increase of 150 percent. La Flor de Cano is primarily a short-filler Cuban brand, but it occasionally takes premium form as a Regional Edition cigar.
