Fonseca Du Nord: Canada's First Regional Edition of the Plain-Packaging Era

The Genios-format (52 x 140 mm) Fonseca Du Nord launched across Toronto, Montreal, Winnipeg and Vancouver in January 2026, the first Habanos Regional Edition for Canada since plain packaging took effect.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
January 20, 2026
Release
Launch week January 20-25, 2026 (Toronto, Montreal, Winnipeg and Vancouver)
Maker
Habanos S.A.

The Fonseca Du Nord, a new Edación Regional created exclusively for Canada, was officially launched during the week of January 20–25, 2026, with celebrations in four cities — Toronto, Montreal, Winnipeg and Vancouver — Habanos S.A. announced. The cigar uses the Genios factory vitola (52 x 140 mm) and is made totally by hand with long filler, using wrapper, filler and binder leaves from Vuelta Abajo in Pinar del Río.

The launch is a milestone for the market: the Du Nord is the first Regional Edition for Canada since plain-packaging legislation came into force there, according to Habanos, and it reaches smokers through Havana House Cigar and Tobacco Merchants Ltd., the exclusive distributor. Around 200 guests attended the four launch events, which opened in Toronto on January 20 and closed in Vancouver on January 25.
