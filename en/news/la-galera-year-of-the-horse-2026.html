La Galera's Year of the Horse 2026 Puts 12 Toro Gordos and Three Accessories in a Numbered Box

Tabacalera Palma's zodiac series continues with a 6 x 56 toro gordo for the Year of the Horse, sold in a four-drawer red-and-gold box of 12 cigars with a cutter, lighter and ashtray for $690, limited to 2,026 numbered boxes.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
February 4, 2026
Release
February 4, 2026
Maker
La Galera (Tabacalera Palma)

La Galera has marked Chinese New Year with a zodiac release for several years, and for 2026 the Dominican brand's entry is the La Galera Year of the Horse Limited Edition 2026. The cigar is a 6 x 56 toro gordo — the company corrected an initial announcement that had listed it at 5 x 56 — timed for the lunar new year, which fell on Feb. 17.

The blend uses a Brazilian Arapiraca wrapper over a Dominican olor binder and four Dominican fillers: criollo 98, olor, pelo de oro and piloto cubano. It is made at Tabacalera Palma, José Arnaldo Blanco's factory in the Dominican Republic.

The packaging does much of the talking. The red-and-gold box, decorated with a horse and palm trees in a nod to Tabacalera Palma, holds 12 cigars in drawers that pull out from all four sides, plus three accessories — a cutter, a lighter and an ashtray — stored at the base. Boxes are priced at $690, and production is limited to 2,026 numbered boxes, matching the year.
