Boveda Announces a 100-Count Humidor, on Sale November 17 for $399

Boveda, best known for its two-way humidity control, has announced a wooden desktop humidor holding 100 cigars — a second model that doubles the capacity of its award-winning 50-count humidor while keeping the same seasoning-free system. It goes on sale November 17 at $399, exclusively through Boveda's official store.

Boveda, the company best known for its two-way humidity-control packs, has announced a wooden desktop humidor, the Boveda 100-Count Humidor. It is the second model in the line, doubling the capacity of the company's award-winning 50-count humidor while using the same seasoning-free system.

The humidor has a Macassar ebony finish and a Spanish cedar interior, with removable dividers and a built-in Boveda holder. Four 84% seasoning packs and two 69% maintenance packs are included.

It goes on sale on November 17, priced at $399 and sold exclusively through Boveda's official store, timed for the holiday season. A one-year limited warranty is included.
