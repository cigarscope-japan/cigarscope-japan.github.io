Spain Seizes 33,840 Counterfeit Cuban Cigars Near Valencia

The Guardia Civil seized 33,840 fake Cuban cigars at a residence in Mislata, Valencia, where they were being fitted with counterfeit bands and packaged for distribution throughout Europe, Cigar Journal reports.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
July 13, 2026
Maker
Habanos S.A.

Spain's Guardia Civil has seized 33,840 counterfeit Cuban cigars at a residence in Mislata, Valencia, in eastern Spain, Cigar Journal reports. At the residence, the cigars were being fitted with counterfeit bands and packaged for distribution throughout Europe.

The operation points to the counterfeit trade that shadows genuine Habanos in Europe: the cigars were seized at the preparation stage, while counterfeit bands were being applied and the stock packaged for distribution throughout Europe.
