Reblended Eiroa Dark 30th Reaches Stores, Now With Colombian Pinareño

CLE Cigar Co.'s Eiroa Dark has returned as the Eiroa Dark 30th, marking Christian Eiroa's 30 years in the industry: reblended after more than a year, with Colombian-grown pinareño joining the family's corojo, in four sizes at $18.06 to $20.26.

C.L.E. Cigar Co.

CLE Cigar Co.'s Eiroa Dark 30th arrived at U.S. stores on June 26. More than a year ago, the company announced that the Eiroa Dark would be reblended and get updated packaging; the end result, after more than a year of reblending and with an extra band added, is this 30th anniversary line, which commemorates founder Christian Eiroa's 30 years in the cigar industry.

The original Eiroa Dark was an all-Honduran cigar built on the Eiroa family's corojo. The 30th is a reinterpretation of it: Christian Eiroa says the project was an exercise in chasing a taste from his past, and the new blend adds pinareño-seed tobacco grown on the company's farm in Colombia alongside the family's signature corojo.

The line is offered in four sizes, all in 10-count boxes — the format the Eiroa lines are moving to: 50 x 5 ($18.06), the 11/18 (6 x 48/54/48, $19.26), 54 x 6 ($19.26) and 60 x 6 ($20.26).
