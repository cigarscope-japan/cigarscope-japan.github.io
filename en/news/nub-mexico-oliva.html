Oliva's NUb México Pairs a Mexican Wrapper With Nicaraguan Binder and Filler

Oliva is adding a Mexican-wrapped line to the NUb family. The NUb México comes in the 460 and the U.S.-exclusive 464T torpedo, debuted at InterTabac 2026 in Dortmund and was released in the U.S. on October 5.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
September 14, 2026
Release
October 5, 2026
Maker
Oliva

A new version of Oliva Cigar Co.'s NUb line is debuting in Germany ahead of its arrival in the U.S. The NUb México uses a Mexican-grown wrapper, while the binder and filler are both Nicaraguan — a combination the company reportedly describes as medium in body and slightly spicy. The cigars are made at Tabacalera Oliva in Nicaragua.

The line is released in two sizes, named for their dimensions: the 460 (4 x 60, $10.83) and the 464T (4 x 64, $11.16), the latter a torpedo. Both come in 24-count boxes. The 460 will be available to all markets, while the 464T is slated to be exclusive to U.S. retailers. The U.S. release followed on October 5, 2026.

The NUb México joins the NUb Habano, NUb Maduro, NUb Cameroon and NUb Connecticut as the brand's core lines, nearly two decades after the short, thick NUb concept debuted in 2008. The new line was on display at the InterTabac trade fair in Dortmund, Germany, held September 15-17, 2026.
