BVI Court Move Could Return Chen Zhi's Habanos Stake to His Companies

Tobacco Reporter says a BVI High Court decision could restore control over entities tied to a 28.55% interest in Habanos — while Chen denies the allegations and separate U.S. and EU measures continue.

Published
November 8, 2026
Announced
November 6, 2026
Maker
Habanos S.A.

A British Virgin Islands court decision could hand control of a major Habanos shareholding back to companies linked to Chinese businessman Chen Zhi, Tobacco Reporter reports. Justice Gerhard Wallbank ruled that liquidation orders covering more than 30 Chen-linked entities should be discharged because the BVI Attorney General had failed to provide admissible evidence supporting the applications.

Among the entities is Simply Advanced Ltd., which holds Chen's interest in Allied Cigar Corporation, the Madrid-based company that owns 50% of Habanos S.A., Cuba's premium cigar exporter; Tobacco Reporter puts Chen's stake in Habanos at 28.55%. Court-appointed liquidators from Interpath had taken control of the entities in January. The ruling could halt plans to sell Chen's interests, although further legal proceedings in the United States and the BVI could affect the outcome, and the future ownership remains unresolved.

The other side

Chen has denied the allegations against him. “Chen has denied the allegations through a Prince Group spokesperson,” Tobacco Reporter reports — the allegations being U.S. and British claims that he was involved in a multibillion-dollar criminal enterprise, which set the liquidation in motion. Allied Cigar also holds two factories outside Cuba and the Vegafina and Flor de Copan brands, so the outcome reaches beyond the Cuban cigar trade itself.

Editorial note

This story rests on secondary reporting. CigarScope Japan has not reviewed the underlying court filings, and what happens next — including any effect on a possible sale of the stake — remains unconfirmed.
