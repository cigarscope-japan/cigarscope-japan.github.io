Drew Estate Releases Event-Only Liga Privada Crush'd Velvet

Drew Estate has released the Liga Privada Único Serie Crush'd Velvet, an event-only 5 x 50 box-pressed belicoso at $16.95, which debuted at the Florida Barn Smoker and goes on sale through selected retailers' events.

Maker

Drew Estate has released the Liga Privada Único Serie Crush'd Velvet, an event-only 5 x 50 box-pressed belicoso, Halfwheel reported. The blend uses a U.S. Connecticut corojo hybrid wrapper, a Mexican binder and fillers from Nicaragua and the United States, and is rolled at La Gran Fábrica Drew Estate in Estelí.

The cigar debuted at the Florida Barn Smoker, the Drew Estate event co-hosted with Corona Cigar Co., which served as the launch account when the cigar went on sale on November 6. It will also be sold at events hosted by selected catalogue retailers, including Best Cigar Pub, Casa de Montecristo and Cigars International. Pricing is $16.95 per cigar and $169.50 for a box of 10.

Despite the similar name, Drew Estate says the Crush'd Velvet and the Velvet Rat — another Único Serie release that began as an event cigar in 2018 — are very different blends.
