Joya de Nicaragua Cinco de Cinco Corona Doble Scores 90 — the Anniversary Line's Longest Size Reaches the U.S.

Cigar Coop has reviewed the Corona Doble of Joya de Nicaragua's Cinco de Cinco, the 55th anniversary line from Nicaragua's oldest cigar factory, and scored it 90 points — the magazine's Standard of Excellence — with a "Buy One" verdict. The Corona Doble, which joined the U.S. market in 2026, is a 7 x 54 soft box-pressed cigar using a Mexican San Andrés wrapper and Nicaraguan fillers (binder undisclosed) described as ultra-vintage leaf. The blend traces back to blending sessions held for visitors to the factory. It is $21 per cigar, and Coop has now scored all three sizes it has reviewed in the line at 90 points, ranking the Corona Doble second among them.

According to Cigar Coop, the Corona Doble of Joya de Nicaragua's Cinco de Cinco — the 55th anniversary line from Nicaragua's oldest cigar factory — has been reviewed after joining the U.S. market in 2026, scoring 90 points, the magazine's Standard of Excellence, with a "Buy One" verdict. The 7 x 54 soft box-pressed cigar uses a Mexican San Andrés wrapper and Nicaraguan fillers, with the binder undisclosed, in what is described as an ultra-vintage blend. The blend was developed from records of leaves chosen by visitors during blending experiences at the factory.

The flavors centered on chocolate, fruit, mineral, earth and pepper, with burn and draw both rated excellent, deepening from medium to medium-full in the final third, Cigar Coop reported. Pricing is $21 per cigar. Coop has scored all three sizes it has reviewed in the line at 90 points, and ranked the Corona Doble second among them.
