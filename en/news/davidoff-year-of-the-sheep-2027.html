Davidoff Year of the Sheep 2027: All Three Formats Are Figurados

For the first time in the zodiac series' history, all three formats are figurados in tribute to a sheep's horn — a 17,500-box piramides, an 850-box flagship gran perfecto, and a 12-piece Masterpiece Humidor.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
November 2, 2026
Release
November 19, 2026
Maker
Davidoff

Davidoff has announced the Year of the Sheep Limited Edition 2027, the next instalment of its Chinese zodiac series — and for the first time in the series' history, all three formats are figurados, their tapering shapes a tribute to the curve of a sheep's horn. "Bringing three figurado formats to life is both a statement of our craftsmanship and a tribute to the harmony this zodiac sign represents," said Javier González, the company's SVP Head of Global Marketing & Innovation.

The main release is the piramides, 52 ring gauge by 6 3/4 inches, with an Ecuadorian wrapper over fillers from the Dominican Republic and Nicaragua, presented in ten-count boxes of dark-toned solid wood with a wool-like fabric lining. Production is limited to 17,500 boxes worldwide. Sharing the same blend, a gran perfecto (61 by 6 inches) will be sold only at Davidoff of Geneva since 1911 flagship stores, limited to 850 boxes.

At the top of the collection sits the Masterpiece Humidor, which holds 88 gran torpedo cigars and is limited to just 12 pieces worldwide; its artwork, designed by Chinese illustrator Jane Liu, was translated into embroidery on silk satin by French textile artist Clémentine Brandibas, in more than one hundred hours of handwork per piece. The collection goes on sale from November 19, 2026, at appointed merchants and flagship stores, with launch dates varying by country.
