New Release · Dominican Republic

Broncano Adds a Smaller Corona to Its Classic Rubí Line

Broncano is adding the Classic Rubí Corona, a 5 x 46 corona with a pigtail cap made at Kelner Boutique Factory in the Dominican Republic, with the first shipments reserved for the retailers that supported the brand early on.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
November 8, 2026
Release
Shipping now (reported November 8, 2026); expected at select retailers in mid-to-late November 2026
Maker
Broncano Cigar Co.

Broncano Cigar Co. is adding a smaller size to its flagship Classic Rubí line: the Classic Rubí Corona, a 5 x 46 corona with a pigtail cap, Cigar Dojo reported. It is produced at Kelner Boutique Factory in the Dominican Republic, with an HVA Ecuador wrapper over a Dominican San Vicente Seco binder and fillers of Dominican Criollo 98 and Piloto Cubano, Mexican San Andrés Morron and U.S. broadleaf; the company describes the strength as medium-plus.

Founder and president Martin Smith says the smaller format raises the wrapper-to-filler ratio compared with the larger Classic Rubí sizes, giving the blend a different balance while remaining unmistakably Rubí. The Corona comes in a four-count sleeve-and-tray box priced at $50, equivalent to $12.50 per cigar; production numbers were not disclosed.

Rather than starting with a broad rollout, the initial shipments are going to select retailers that supported Broncano early on. The cigars are shipping now, with arrival at those stores expected in mid-to-late November.
