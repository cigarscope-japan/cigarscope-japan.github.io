Oliva Serie V Melanio Edicion Ano 2026 Arrives With an Unusual Perfecto Shape

The 2026 edition of Oliva's annual limited vitola — a 5 3/8 x 54 shape that is half parejo, half perfecto, built around the warm, rainy 2023-24 Jalapa harvest — reached U.S. stores on November 2, limited to 9,000 boxes at $20.

Published

Maker
Oliva

One of the most interesting-looking vitolas in the history of the Oliva Serie V Melanio line has begun arriving at stores. The new Edición Año 2026 is a 5 3/8 x 54 perfecto whose top half looks like a fairly standard parejo while the bottom half has a fairly sharp taper — a silhouette similar to the Oliva Serie V 135th Anniversary Edición Limitada, and an unusual shape even within the annual limited-vitola series that has run since 2017.

As interesting as the shape is, Oliva is more interested in talking about the tobacco inside. The wrapper is an Ecuadorian Sumatra-seed leaf, but the tobacco inside comes from the 2023-2024 crop in Jalapa, Nicaragua, which was warm and rainy and produced an exceptional harvest that the company likens to a grand cru in the wine world. "The Oliva Serie V Melanio is already the top of the top," said Fred Vandermarliere, CEO of Vandermarliere Cigar Family, the parent company of Oliva, "but this limited edition takes the experience to a level one can only dream about, as these special conditions cannot be man-made; they can only be handed down by nature."

The cigar is made at Tabacalera Oliva de Nicaragua and priced at $20 per cigar, $200 for a box of ten. Production is limited to 9,000 boxes — 90,000 cigars — of which 5,000 boxes are destined for markets outside the United States. It arrived at U.S. stores on November 2.
