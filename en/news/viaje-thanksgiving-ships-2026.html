Viaje's Two Thanksgiving Cigars Ship Today, With an Unusual Plea to Pass the Savings to Consumers

Viaje's two Thanksgiving regulars ship today, November 16. The Stuffed Turkey Edición Limitada repacks the familiar blend as a box-pressed 5 x 58, while the new One Happy Family is a 6 x 56 toro gordo. Viaje is offering the Stuffed Turkey to retailers at a special price and asking them to pass the discount on to consumers. Blend details, pricing and production numbers have not been disclosed.

Published
November 16, 2026
Announced
November 13, 2026
Maker
Viaje

According to CigarMind, Viaje's two Thanksgiving regulars ship today, November 16 (first reported in our November 14 edition). One is the Stuffed Turkey Edición Limitada: the familiar blend repacked as a box-pressed 5 x 58, with the same Nicaraguan binder and filler as the regular Stuffed Turkey and a Mexican San Andrés wrapper. The other, the new One Happy Family, is a 6 x 56 toro gordo. Neither cigar's blend details, pricing nor production numbers have been disclosed.

A further twist: Viaje is said to be offering the Stuffed Turkey to retailers at a special price and asking them to pass the discount on to consumers. Retail prices will therefore vary by shop, and finding a shop that has followed the request could make this year's Thanksgiving cigar a better deal than usual. [Sources: CigarMind (Nov 13, 2026); primary reporting Halfwheel (Nov 12, 2026)]
