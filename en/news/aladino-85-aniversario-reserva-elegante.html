Aladino 85 Aniversario Reserva Elegante Ships in the Week of November 9, a 7 x 38 Lancero at $22

JRE Tobacco has begun shipping the Aladino 85 Aniversario Reserva Elegante in the week of November 9 — a 7 x 38 lancero and third size in the 85 Aniversario Reserva line, at $22 per cigar and $440 per box of 20, offered only to qualifying retailers.

Week of November 9, 2026 (shipping)
Maker
JRE Tobacco (Aladino)

JRE Tobacco has begun shipping a new size in its Aladino 85 Aniversario Reserva line, the Elegante, in the week of November 9. The Elegante is a 7 x 38 lancero and the third vitola in the line, which was unveiled at the 2026 PCA trade show and marks company founder Julio R. Eiroa's 85th anniversary.

Wrapper, binder and filler are all Honduran pinareño-seed tobacco, a lighter seed brought from Cuba to Central America at around the same time as corojo but long overshadowed by it. The cigars are made at the Fábrica de Puros Aladino at Las Lomas Jamastran in Honduras.

The Elegante is regular production, but distribution is deliberately narrow: it is offered only to stores that carry both the 85 Aniversario Reserva Robusto and Toro, and those stores are limited to two boxes per month. Pricing is $22 per cigar (about ¥3,510) and $440 for a box of 20 (about ¥70,200).
