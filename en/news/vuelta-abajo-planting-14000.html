Tabacuba Aims Again for 14,000 Hectares in the Vuelta Abajo for 2026-27

Tabacuba hopes to plant 14,000 hectares in the Vuelta Abajo next season — the same target missed in 2025-26 — leaning on a rapid build-out of solar-powered irrigation in Pinar del Río.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
June 4, 2026
Maker
Habanos S.A.

Tabacuba hopes to plant 14,000 hectares of tobacco next season in the Vuelta Abajo, Cuba’s main tobacco-growing region, according to a report from the official Cuban news agency ACN carried by Cigars-connect. The target is the same one set before the 2025–26 season — and missed, after a season in which plantings rose only modestly on the year before.

The plan leans on solar power. The Tabacuba group hopes to have 50 percent of its irrigated land in Pinar del Río province powered by renewable energy. The province already has 449 solar systems installed, with another 600 being installed, according to Osvaldo Santana Vera, Tabacuba’s agricultural director; some 1,500 more pumps of this type are expected to arrive in the country between August and September, before planting begins in October.

Since Hurricane Ian destroyed almost all of the region’s drying facilities in September 2022, Tabacuba’s strategy has been to increase planted area by about 2,000 hectares per year, worth an increase of approximately 1,000 tons of harvest per season. Pinar del Río supplies more than 70 percent of the tobacco produced in Cuba, including all of its filler and binder tobaccos.
