Miami Cigar & Co. Signs National Distribution Deal With Arango Cigar Co.

Miami Cigar & Company has announced a distribution partnership with Arango Cigar Co., the wholesaler with more than nine decades in the trade, putting brands including Tatiana into Arango's national network of retailers.

Published
November 11, 2026
Announced
November 9, 2026
Maker
Miami Cigar & Co.

Miami Cigar & Company has announced a distribution partnership with Arango Cigar Co., one of the United States' most established wholesale distributors of premium cigars — as well as European pipes, pipe tobacco, cigarette tobacco and smoking accessories — Smokin Tabacco reported. The deal strengthens Miami Cigar's national distribution network and widens the availability of its portfolio, which includes the flavoured Tatiana brand, to retailers across the country.

Arango brings more than nine decades of experience in the tobacco industry. "Bringing Arango into our distribution network is something we've been working toward for quite some time," said Jason Wood of Miami Cigar & Company, adding that it mattered to align with a partner sharing the company's commitment to relationships, service and long-term growth. Corey Gold of Arango said his company was proud to partner with "another family-owned company" — both businesses are family owned.
