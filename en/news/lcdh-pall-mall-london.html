La Casa del Habano Opens Pall Mall Flagship in London

Dominique London opened its La Casa del Habano flagship at 26 Pall Mall on March 25, with two walk-in humidors, a basement lounge for rare and vintage cigars, and four opening parties.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
April 1, 2026
Maker
Habanos S.A.

La Casa del Habano Pall Mall opened on March 25 at 26 Pall Mall in London’s St James’s district, a flagship boutique that Dominique Gyselinck, founder of operator Dominique London, describes as the company’s “crown jewel,” Cigars-connect reports. No fewer than four opening parties followed the official opening, drawing shareholders, partners and leading figures of the London cigar scene.

On the ground floor, an accessories corner sits beside a large walk-in humidor designed by the Italian house DeArt; in the basement, a second, smaller humidor holds rare and vintage cigars in front of a smoking lounge that seats about a dozen. Dominique London runs more than thirty locations in seven countries — the largest network of cigar shops in Europe — and was preparing to open its 33rd establishment, in Luxembourg, within two months of the report.
