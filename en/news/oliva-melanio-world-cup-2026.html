Oliva Packs 24 Melanio Double Toros Into a Soccer-Ball Box for the 2026 World Cup

For the 2026 World Cup, Oliva is releasing the Serie V Melanio Gran Reserva Limitada World Cup 2026 Edition: a soccer-ball-shaped box holding 12 Melanio Double Toros and 12 Melanio Maduro Double Toros, limited to 5,000 boxes at $680.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
April 6, 2026
Release
Shipping to stores following PCA 2026 (April 18-20, 2026)
Maker
Oliva

A few cigar companies have announced special releases for the 2026 World Cup, but so far only one has created a soccer ball. Oliva Cigar Co. has announced the Oliva Serie V Melanio Gran Reserva Limitada World Cup 2026 Edition, a box shaped like a soccer ball that comes with 24 cigars. Inside are the regular production Serie V Melanio Double Toro — 12 of the standard blend and 12 of the Maduro. Both measure 6 x 60 and are box-pressed, and while they are regular production cigars, the ones used for this release carry a special secondary band.

Each box has an MSRP of $680, and production is limited to 5,000 boxes, with 4,250 of them slated for the American market. The sets are expected to ship to stores following the 2026 PCA Convention & Trade Show, which takes place April 18-20 in New Orleans.

"Everybody sees the goals, everybody hears the cheers, but the true power of legends lies in the way they took the gift of nature and turned it into a craft — and the craft itself is a new gift to the world," said Fred Vandermarliere, owner of Oliva Cigar Co., in a press release. The 2026 World Cup kicks off on Thursday, June 11, in Mexico; OZ Family Cigars and Plasencia have also announced releases inspired by the tournament.
