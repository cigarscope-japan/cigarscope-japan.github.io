My Father's Le Bijou 1922 Gets a Connecticut-Seed Wrapper for a November Limited Edition

My Father is releasing a limited edition of its Le Bijou 1922 line with a Connecticut-seed wrapper, in a robusto and a toro limited to 1,500 boxes each. Shown at the 2026 TAA meeting but not a TAA exclusive, it ships to stores in November.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
October 29, 2026
Release
November 2026 (shipping to stores; a specific date has not been announced)
Maker
My Father Cigars

My Father Cigars has announced a limited edition version of its Le Bijou 1922 line that uses a Connecticut-seed wrapper. The core line, which debuted in 2009, uses a dark, Nicaraguan habano oscuro wrapper over a Nicaraguan binder and filler; it was created by José "Pepín" García as a tribute to his father, José García Alayón, who was born in 1922 — Le Bijou is French for "the jewel". Full details of the new blend are not being released, but the limited edition is expected to wear a much lighter-coloured wrapper consistent with Connecticut-seed leaf, and no product images had been released at the time of the announcement.

A Connecticut-wrapped Le Bijou has a precedent: in 2012, My Father created one for Federal Cigar's 91st anniversary, a 6 1/2 x 42 lonsdale that used an Ecuadorian-grown, Connecticut-seed wrapper, and more sizes of that blend followed in subsequent years in very limited amounts. The company did not offer a comparison between the new cigar and the Federal Cigar blend.

For this release, My Father is offering two sizes — a robusto (5 1/4 x 52, $12) and a toro (6 x 54, $13) — both in 20-count boxes, with production limited to 1,500 boxes of each size. The cigars were made at the company's factory in Estelí, Nicaragua, and were shown at the 2026 Tobacconists' Association of America meeting, though the release is not exclusive to the organisation's retail members. They are slated to ship to stores in November, with a specific date yet to be announced.
