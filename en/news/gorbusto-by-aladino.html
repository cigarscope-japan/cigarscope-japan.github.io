Camp Aladino's Trio Returns as Gorbusto by Aladino, Fronted by Three Primate Characters

JRE Tobacco has renamed last year's Camp Aladino blends as Gorbusto by Aladino: the same 4 1/2 x 60 cigars at $11, now represented by the primate characters GORI, CHIMP and ORANG, arriving in April.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
March 30, 2026
Release
April 2026 (expected to arrive on shelves; shown at PCA 2026, April 18-20)
Maker
JRE Tobacco (Aladino)

Last year, JRE Tobacco Co. introduced a trio of cigars under its Camp Aladino line, each in a 4 1/2 x 60 vitola the company calls Gorbusto — a hybrid of gordo and robusto. The cigars will continue to be sold, but no longer under the Camp Aladino name: going forward, the three blends are known as Gorbusto by Aladino.

The old line distinguished its blends by wrapper — Cameroon, Corojo and Maduro. The new line instead gives each blend a primate character: GORI, the alpha, "bold strength, full presence"; CHIMP, the thinker, "refined depth, quiet complexity"; and ORANG, the creator, "creative character, unexpected notes." The company is not disclosing which Camp Aladino blend corresponds to which character, but the blends themselves are unchanged, and the filler blend remains undisclosed.

JRE is keeping the MSRP at $11 per cigar, in boxes of 20. The new branding was shown to retailers at the 2026 PCA Convention & Trade Show, held April 18-20 in New Orleans, with the cigars expected on shelves in April. The Camp Aladino name is not going away: a new blend and a new vitola for that line are planned for the summer.
