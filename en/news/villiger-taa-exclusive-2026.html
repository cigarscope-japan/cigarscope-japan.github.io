Villiger TAA Exclusive Returns for 2026, Made at the Company's Own Nicaraguan Factory

After a year off, Villiger's TAA Exclusive is a 6 x 52 box-pressed toro from Villiger de Nicaragua, limited to 500 boxes of ten at $13 per cigar and released on November 2.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
October 14, 2026
Release
November 2, 2026
Maker
Villiger

After taking a year off for 2025, the Villiger TAA Exclusive Series returns for 2026 — and for the first time, the cigar is made at Villiger's own Nicaraguan factory, Villiger de Nicaragua. From 2020 to 2024, Villiger used its TAA Exclusive releases to work with different factories, five in total across three countries; the 2026 edition brings the series home as a showcase for the company's own rollers.

The Villiger TAA Exclusive 2026 is a 6 x 52 box-pressed toro with an Ecuadorian habano rosado wrapper, a Mexican San Andrés binder and Nicaraguan fillers, a blend the company describes as medium-to-full-bodied. It was released on November 2, with an MSRP of $13 per cigar, limited to 500 boxes of ten — 5,000 cigars in total.

The cigars are sold only through members of the Tobacconists' Association of America (TAA), a membership group of around 80 retailers and approximately 40 manufacturers. Under the TAA Exclusive Series Program, participating manufacturers agree to give a portion of the proceeds to the organization, a minimum of 50 cents per cigar.
