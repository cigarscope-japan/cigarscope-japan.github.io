Domain Cigars Opens a Test Blend Portal for Its Flagship Retailers

Domain's flagship retailers and their customers can now log flavour notes, construction and burn feedback — plus a personal score out of 100 — on unreleased blends from Esteban Disla and Kevin Baxter, with the notes going straight to the blenders.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
November 2, 2026
Maker
Domain Cigars

Domain Cigars has set up a Test Blend Portal for its flagship retailers, so that retailers — and, through them, their customers — can smoke unreleased test blends and send their impressions back to the people who made them. The test blends are the product of blending sessions between Esteban Disla and Kevin Baxter.

The process is simple. After receiving and smoking a test blend, a reviewer logs on to the portal, looks up the blend and records flavour notes, construction, performance and tasting comments on a feedback screen; the system also calculates a personal numerical rating from 1 to 100. "Every test blend now has a dedicated review experience. When you smoke one and share your thoughts, those notes go directly to Kevin and Esteban. Our master blenders can read your feedback and consider it as they continue developing new products," said Domain Cigars co-founder Daniel Lance.

The cigars are available exclusively through a handful of flagship retailers, who receive them first and decide how to share them with their customers. "No elaborate launch. No promises that these cigars will ever become commercial products. Just an opportunity to experience the work happening inside our factory and share your thoughts with the people making them," Lance added.
