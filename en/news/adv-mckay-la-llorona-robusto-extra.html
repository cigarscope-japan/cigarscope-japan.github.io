ADV & McKay Ships La Llorona Robusto Extra, Limited to 1,000 Boxes for Its Top 20 Retailers

ADV & McKay Cigars Co. has begun shipping the La Llorona Robusto Extra, a 5 3/4 x 52 fourth size for the Dominican line, limited to 1,000 boxes of 10 cigars sold only to the company's top 20 retailers at $17 per cigar.

ADV & McKay Cigars Co. has begun shipping the La Llorona Robusto Extra, a 5 3/4 x 52 fourth size for the Dominican line, Halfwheel reported. The blend keeps the line's formula — an Ecuadorian habano wrapper over binders from Indonesia and the Dominican Republic with Dominican fillers — and is rolled at the company's own Tabacalera Mina del Rey in Tamboril.

Production is limited to 1,000 boxes of 10 cigars (10,000 cigars in total), sold only to the company's top 20 retailers. Pricing is set at $17 per cigar and $170 per box. The Robusto Extra, released on November 9, joins the Robusto (2022), the Corona (November 2023) and the Toro (October 2025); the Corona placed #24 on Halfwheel's Top 25 Cigars of 2023.

The line takes its name from La Llorona, the "weeping woman" of Latin American folklore. ADV & McKay tweaked the legend for its version: instead of a weeping spirit, its La Llorona hunts pirates.
