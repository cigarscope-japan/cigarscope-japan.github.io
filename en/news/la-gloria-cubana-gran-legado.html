Forged Brings Back La Gloria Cubana Gran Legado for a Limited 2026 Run

The 7 3/4 x 62 perfecto, first released in 2024 and carrying the signature of the veteran El Credito roller who made it, returns for 2026 in ten-count boxes at $15.89.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
November 3, 2026
Release
Limited 2026 run, announced November 3, 2026
Maker
La Gloria Cubana

Forged Cigar Company has brought back the La Gloria Cubana Gran Legado for a limited 2026 run. First introduced in 2024, the Gran Legado — the name translates to "great legacy" — is a massive 7 3/4 x 62 perfecto inspired by the popular La Gloria Cubana Serie R, with a Connecticut Broadleaf wrapper over higher primings of Dominican and Nicaraguan tobaccos. This is the Dominican-made La Gloria Cubana sold by STG's Forged Cigar Company, not the Cuban brand of the same name.

The cigar is created at El Credito, a "factory within a factory" located at General Cigar Dominicana in the Dominican Republic, where some of the brand's most seasoned rollers and bunchers hand-roll it. Each member of the Gran Legado team has more than 15 years of experience working with La Gloria Cubana, and each cigar bears the signature of the team member who made it.

"We decided to bring Gran Legado back not only because fans kept asking, but to continue to tell the story of El Credito and the legacy of our rollers who are family at this point," said John Hakim, brand manager for La Gloria Cubana. The Gran Legado comes in ten-count boxes and has an MSRP of $15.89 per cigar.
