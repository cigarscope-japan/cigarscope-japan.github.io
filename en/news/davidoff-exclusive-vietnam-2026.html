Davidoff's First Vietnam Exclusive Edition: 300 Numbered Boxes of Perfectos

Davidoff presented its first special edition for Vietnam in Ho Chi Minh City on March 30, marking five years with the Avanti Group. The 155mm x 53 perfecto is limited to 300 numbered boxes of 10 at €85 per cigar.

Davidoff has presented its first special edition created for Vietnam. The Davidoff Exclusive Edition 2026 Vietnam was unveiled on March 30 at the Avanti Group's flagship store in Ho Chi Minh City, at a launch party that also celebrated the fifth anniversary of the partnership between Davidoff and the Avanti Group, which began in 2021.

The cigar is a perfecto of 155mm x 53, a sophisticated shape chosen as a nod to the lotus petal, the national flower of Vietnam. Its medium-bodied blend combines tobaccos from the Dominican Republic, Ecuador, Nicaragua and the United States, selected around the preferences of Vietnamese smokers. Only 300 numbered boxes of 10 cigars will be available, sold exclusively through the 11 Habanos Specialist boutiques in Vietnam at €85 per cigar (about $99).

Attending the launch, Oettinger Davidoff CEO Beat Hauenstein said the group's sales in Vietnam have increased 56-fold since the partnership started. Avanti Group CEO Sami Alrayhani also revealed that the first Davidoff boutique in Vietnam will open in Ho Chi Minh City in a few months, with a second planned for Hanoi next year.
