La Aurora Revives Its Rum-Barrel Double Barrel Aged With a New Blend

The 2016 limited edition, withdrawn in 2019 over tobacco quality concerns, returns in the same 5 x 54 double perfecto with about half the blend changed, swapping Brazilian leaf for Pennsylvania broadleaf; 750 boxes of eight at $28.50.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
April 2, 2026
Release
Late April 2026 (on shelves; shown at PCA 2026, April 18-20)
Maker
La Aurora

La Aurora is bringing back its rum-barrel-aged Preferidos 1903 Edition Double Barrel Aged, first released as a limited edition in November 2016. The original run had an unusual history: the factory grew dissatisfied with some of the tobaccos in the blend, and by March 2019 the cigar had been pulled from production.

The 2026 version keeps the same format — the 5 x 54 double perfecto the company calls Preferidos, sold in brown metal tubes — and the same Ecuadorian habano wrapper, but roughly half the blend has changed. The Brazilian binder and Brazilian filler tobaccos of 2016 are replaced by Pennsylvania broadleaf, joining Dominican and Nicaraguan fillers. True to the name, the binder and fillers are aged in barrels that once held E. León Jimenes rum, and the finished cigars are then aged in the barrels a second time. The easiest way to tell the two versions apart: the 2026 band drops the silver elements of the original.

Each cigar carries an MSRP of $28.50, and production is limited to 750 boxes of eight. The cigars were shown at the 2026 PCA Convention & Trade Show in April and reached shelves in late April. "The tobaccos behind this cigar are not merely aged — they are transformed," said master blender Manuel Inoa.
