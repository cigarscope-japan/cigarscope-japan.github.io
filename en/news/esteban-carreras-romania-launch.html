Esteban Carreras Launches in Romania With a Bucharest Event

Esteban Carreras has entered the Romanian market, with importer Smo'Kars hosting a launch event at the Suter Palace Hotel in Bucharest attended by Christopher Mey, the company's managing director for international business.

Published
November 12, 2026
Announced
November 10, 2026
Maker
Esteban Carreras

Esteban Carreras has entered the Romanian market. Its local importer, Smo'Kars, hosted a launch event at the Suter Palace Hotel in Bucharest, attended by Christopher Mey, managing director of Esteban Carreras International, as well as cigar enthusiasts and industry representatives.

The launch formally brings the company's brands into Romania and is the latest step in its international expansion, extending its reach in Eastern Europe.

Esteban Carreras is owned by Craig Cunningham, and its cigars are handcrafted at the company's Tabacalera Carreras factory in Estelí, Nicaragua. The brand is named for Esteban Carreras, a Cuban freedom fighter.
