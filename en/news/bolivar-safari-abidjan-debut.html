Bolívar "Safari" Debuts in Abidjan Today — Africa's First Regional Edition

Africa's first Edición Regional, the Bolívar "Safari," will be unveiled today, November 21, in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire. Created to mark Bolívar's 125th anniversary, the cigar is a petit robusto of 110 mm x 54 — the Torres vitola — and it is the first Bolívar with a pigtail cap, while the 12-count box is also a first for the brand. Production is limited to 16,666 boxes (about 200,000 cigars), priced at €43 per cigar and €516 per box. In Côte d'Ivoire it goes on advance sale today at Zino Cigares stores, reaching other African countries from January 2027.

Africa's first Edición Regional, the Bolívar "Safari," will be unveiled today, November 21, in Abidjan, Côte d'Ivoire. The venue is the Sofitel Abidjan Hôtel Ivoire, and as the event takes place in the evening local time, photographs and follow-up reports are expected from tomorrow onward. Created to mark Bolívar's 125th anniversary, the cigar is a petit robusto of 110 mm x 54 (4 1/3 x 54) — the Torres vitola, the same as the Trinidad Vigía — and it is the first Bolívar with a pigtail cap, while the 12-count box is also a first for the brand. The secondary band, in red and silver, reads "Edición Regional África 2025." Production is limited to 16,666 boxes (about 200,000 cigars), priced at €43 per cigar and €516 per box. In Côte d'Ivoire it goes on advance sale today at Zino Cigares stores, reaching other African countries from January 2027. The release is a joint venture between Phoenicia T.A.A. Cyprus and local partner Zino Abidjan.
