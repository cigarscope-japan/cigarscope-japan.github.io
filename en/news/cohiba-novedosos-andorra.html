The Near-Lost Cohiba Novedosos Finally Reaches Andorra

Announced in 2019 and stalled by the pandemic, the LCDH-exclusive Novedosos (156 mm x 50) is on sale in Andorra at EUR 5,350 per box of 25, practically its first retail availability worldwide.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
June 2, 2026
Release
On sale in Andorra by June 2, 2026 (Cigars-connect)
Maker
Habanos S.A.

The Cohiba Novedosos, announced in 2019 and reserved for La Casa del Habano franchises and Habanos Specialists, has become available in Andorra — practically the first examples offered for sale worldwide since the start of the Covid-19 pandemic, Cigars-connect reports, citing Maori Tabacs, the exclusive Habanos distributor in the principality.

Presented in Madrid in the fall of 2019, the edition became an indirect victim of the pandemic, which disrupted Cuban production for months; Habanos S.A. has never disclosed how many were produced, and official distributors in the Benelux countries and Switzerland never released a single box, while a few rare boxes were sold in Germany around the turn of 2025–26. The Novedosos is a grand robusto (156 mm x 50; factory name Robustos No. 2) presented in a satin-black box of 25, offered in Andorra at €5,350 (about $6,200) per box, or €214 per cigar.
