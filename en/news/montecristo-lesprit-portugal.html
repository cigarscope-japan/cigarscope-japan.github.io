Montecristo L'Esprit 'Le Crépuscule' Humidor Presented in Portugal

Empor S.A. presented the Montecristo L'Esprit 'Le Crépuscule' humidor — 50 Preciosos cigars, 200 numbered units — to the Portuguese market at the sixth Empor Habanos Moments on March 5, 2026.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
March 24, 2026
Release
March 5, 2026
Maker
Habanos S.A.

Empor S.A., the exclusive distributor of Habanos in Portugal, presented the Montecristo L’Esprit “Le Crépuscule” humidor to the Portuguese market during the sixth edition of Empor Habanos Moments, held at the distributor’s facilities on March 5, 2026. The professional gathering brought together the country’s Casas del Habano, Habanos Specialists, Habanos Points, Habanos Terrace and Habanos Lounge establishments, Habanos S.A. announced.

The humidor was created by S.T. Dupont in collaboration with Habanos S.A. to commemorate the 150th anniversary of the French maison. It contains 50 exclusive cigars in the Preciosos factory size (50 ring gauge x 145 mm), with worldwide production limited to 200 numbered units. Its design draws inspiration from Alexandre Dumas’ novel The Count of Monte Cristo, evoking twilight as a symbol of transformation and rebirth.

The evening also introduced the Montecristo L’Esprit wine collection — three wines from distinct plots in France’s Roussillon region, created with Domaine Lafage and named for the three life stages of Edmond Dantès: Twilight (Le Crépuscule), Night (La Nuit) and Dawn (L’Aurore) — and saw the presentation of the Cohiba Wide Short cigarillo by Promocigar S.L.
