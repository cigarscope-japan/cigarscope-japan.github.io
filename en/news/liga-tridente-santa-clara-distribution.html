Liga Tridente Names Santa Clara Its Exclusive U.S. Distributor

Liga Tridente, the cigar brand co-owned by entertainer Marlon Wayans, has signed an exclusive U.S. distribution agreement with Santa Clara Inc., part of Tabacalera USA, to bring its brands to premium retailers in major cities across the country.

Published
November 10, 2026
Announced
November 9, 2026
Maker
Liga Tridente

Liga Tridente, the cigar brand launched by entertainer Marlon Wayans and partners Vic Curley and David Gaston, has entered into a distribution agreement with Santa Clara Inc. as its exclusive U.S. distributor, Halfwheel reported. The deal is aimed at expanding the brand's footprint across the United States, bringing its cigars to premium retailers in major cities.

"We built Liga Tridente to be a premium experience, not just a cigar," said Wayans, co-owner of Liga Tridente Cigars LLC. "Partnering with Santa Clara lets us bring that experience to every major city — and I get to meet the fans face to face, cut and light with them, and share something real." As part of the agreement, a select group of premium cigar lounges will host exclusive Cut & Light events and meet-and-greet experiences featuring Wayans, Curley and Gaston.

Santa Clara Inc. is part of Tabacalera USA, which also owns JR Cigar and Casa de Montecristo, two of the largest retail operations in the United States. "We are excited for the opportunity to grow Liga Tridente and expand their brands across the market," said Brad Winstead, chief commercial officer of Tabacalera USA.
