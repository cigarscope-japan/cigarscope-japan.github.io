The Year of the Feral Flying Pig — Herrera Estelí Brazilian Maduro Version Scores 90

Drew Estate's signature Feral Flying Pig vitola (5 3/8 x 60 double perfecto), long sold only as the Liga Privada Único Serie cigar of the same name, has gained 12 new versions in the past few months of 2026. They arrived through the company's DE30 consumer rewards program — a three-count sampler of box-pressed versions on September 28 and a 10-count sampler in October. In Halfwheel's review, the Herrera Estelí Brazilian Maduro version scored 90 points; it uses a Brazilian mata fina wrapper, a U.S. Connecticut broadleaf binder and Nicaraguan fillers. The samplers work out to $49 per cigar, and the cigars are not sold individually.

According to Halfwheel, Drew Estate's signature Feral Flying Pig vitola — a 5 3/8 x 60 double perfecto that debuted at the end of 2011 and was long sold only as the Liga Privada Único Serie cigar of the same name — has gained 12 new versions in the past few months of 2026. This site previously reported the 10-count Feral Flying Pig Collection sampler released in October.

On September 28, a three-count sampler for the company's DE30 consumer rewards program brought box-pressed versions of the Herrera Estelí Brazilian Maduro, Liga Privada H99 and Undercrown Maduro. A 10-count sampler followed in October with eight more new releases, including the Herrera Estelí Brazil and Habano, Nica Rustica Shade and Adobe, Liga Privada T52, Undercrown Shade, El Tigre Dominicano and Undercrown 10.

In Halfwheel's review, the Herrera Estelí Brazilian Maduro version scored 90 points. It uses a Brazilian mata fina wrapper, a U.S. Connecticut broadleaf binder and Nicaraguan fillers, and was described as earthy and complex. The samplers work out to $49 per cigar (listed MSRP $16.33), and the cigars are not sold individually.
