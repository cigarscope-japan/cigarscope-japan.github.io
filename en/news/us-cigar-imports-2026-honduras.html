U.S. Premium Cigar Imports Flat Through August; Honduras Up 11.4%

U.S. imports of handmade cigars rose just 0.6 percent to 282.2 million through August, with Nicaragua flat at 168.7 million, the Dominican Republic down 6.3 percent, and Honduras surging 11.4 percent to 53.3 million.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
November 3, 2026
Maker
Industry statistics (Cigar Association of America data)

Imports of premium, handmade cigars into the United States were essentially flat through August, according to the latest data from the Cigar Association of America reported by Cigar Aficionado: 282.2 million cigars were imported in the first eight months of 2026, up 0.6 percent from the 280.6 million imported in the same period last year.

Nicaragua, the longtime market leader, was flat as well, accounting for 168.7 million of those cigars — just shy of 60 percent of the total. The No. 2 producer, the Dominican Republic, shipped 57.3 million cigars, down 6.3 percent from the year before.

Honduras, by contrast, is surging: its shipments jumped 11.4 percent to 53.3 million cigars, up from 47.9 million in the first eight months of 2025, closing the gap on second place. All other countries combined — most notably Costa Rica — made up the remaining 2.9 million cigars, only about 1 percent of imports.
