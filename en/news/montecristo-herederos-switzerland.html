Six Years On, the Montecristo Herederos Finally Reaches Swiss Shops

Announced at the 2020 Festival del Habano and long feared a pandemic casualty, the Churchill-format Herederos (162 mm x 47) went on sale in Switzerland in July 2026 at CHF 59, reserved for LCDH and Habanos Specialist outlets.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
July 16, 2026
Release
July 21, 2026
Maker
Habanos S.A.

Announced at the Festival del Habano in February 2020 — an edition marking the 30th anniversary of the La Casa del Habano franchise and the 85th anniversary of Montecristo — the Herederos was long thought to have fallen victim to the Covid pandemic. This summer it finally appeared: Cigars-connect reported that the cigar was released in Switzerland in July 2026, reserved for La Casa del Habano and Habanos Specialist outlets, much as the Cohiba Novedosos from the 2019 announcements surfaced in Andorra a few weeks earlier.

The Herederos is a Churchill-sized cigar measuring 162 mm in length with a 47 ring gauge. It is presented in humidified boxes of 20 cigars at a recommended price of CHF 59 per stick (approx. €64). The three cigars in the Cigars-connect report went on sale on Tuesday, July 21 in Swiss La Casa del Habano stores, reaching Habanos Specialists a week later.

Two further long-awaited releases arrived alongside it. The Trinidad Villa, a Churchill of 170 mm x 47 in boxes of 12 at CHF 75 per stick, had its world premiere in Mexico last November and is also reserved for LCDH and Specialist outlets. The Cohiba Vistosos, a 145 mm x 53 robusto originally reserved for duty-free shops, joined Swiss retail in a humidified box of 10 at CHF 260 per cigar.
