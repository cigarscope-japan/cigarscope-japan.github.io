La Gloria Cubana 35 Aniversario Humidor Goes on Sale in Switzerland October 6

Marking 35 years of La Casa del Habano rather than of the brand, the 35-cigar Rodolfo humidor (750 made, CHF 3,850) reaches Switzerland's ten LCDH stores on October 6, alongside the Romeo y Julieta Amantes 20-count box.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
September 22, 2026
Release
October 6, 2026
Maker
Habanos S.A.

Nearly 18 months after it debuted at the 2025 Festival del Habano, the La Gloria Cubana 35 Aniversario will go on sale in Switzerland in early October, Halfwheel reported. While the name might suggest an anniversary of the brand, the limited-edition release commemorates 35 years of La Casa del Habano, the worldwide retail network of Cuban cigar stores; it will be sold only at La Casa del Habano retailers, and at the ten in Switzerland retail sales will begin on October 6. There are approximately 155 La Casa del Habano retailers worldwide, in more than 60 countries.

The cigars come in the Rodolfo, a double figurado of 7 1/10 inches (180 mm) with a 47 ring gauge — a size that debuted in 2004 in the San Cristóbal de la Habana 5 Aniversario Humidor as the Muralla. Each humidor contains 35 cigars, with production limited to 750 humidors; in Switzerland, pricing is set at CHF 3,850, or approximately $4,688.

Also arriving in Swiss stores on October 6 is the Romeo y Julieta Amantes in a new 20-count box (6 inches x 53, the Sobresalientes size), priced at CHF 1,288 (about $1,568); the cigar has been available in a smaller five-count box since February 2026, and the larger format will be offered to Habanos Specialist retailers from October 13.
