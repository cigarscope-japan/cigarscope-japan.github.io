Casa Caraballo Releases Elegancia Lanceros in Habano and Maduro

Casa Caraballo has begun shipping two new lanceros under the Elegancia name, a new standalone line that does not belong to any of the company's existing lines. Both are 7 1/2 x 38 lanceros sharing a Nicaraguan binder and fillers; the Habano version uses an Ecuadorian habano wrapper and the Maduro version a Nicaraguan maduro wrapper. Made by Gomez Cigars as regular production, each cigar is $14.50, with 10-count boxes at $145. Shipping began on November 25, 2026.

According to Halfwheel, Casa Caraballo has begun shipping a pair of new lanceros under the Elegancia name. Elegancia is a new standalone line that does not fall under any of the company's existing lines. Both cigars are 7 1/2 x 38 lanceros sharing a Nicaraguan binder and Nicaraguan fillers; one uses an Ecuadorian habano wrapper, the other a Nicaraguan maduro wrapper.

The company says the lancero format's slender profile and higher wrapper-to-filler ratio let the wrapper leaf play a more prominent role in the smoking experience. The cigars are made by Gomez Cigars as regular production. Each cigar is $14.50, with 10-count boxes at $145, and shipping began on November 25, 2026.
