America250 Commission Visits J.C. Newman's El Reloj Factory Today to Honour Its Commemorative Cigars

The United States Semiquincentennial Commission visits J.C. Newman's El Reloj factory in Tampa today, Veterans Day, from 8:00 to 8:30 a.m., to recognise the factory's official America250 commemorative cigars, bringing its custom Airstream trailer along.

Published
November 11, 2026
Announced
November 9, 2026
Maker
J.C. Newman

J.C. Newman Cigar Co. hosts America250 — the United States Semiquincentennial Commission — at its El Reloj factory in Tampa today, Veterans Day, November 11, Cigar Dojo reported. The Commission is scheduled to visit from 8:00 to 8:30 a.m. to recognise J.C. Newman's official America250 commemorative cigars, bringing its custom America250 Airstream trailer for photographs with the factory and the cigars.

The cigars J.C. Newman rolls by hand at El Reloj this year carry the official America250 seal. "J.C. Newman is proud to roll official cigars of the 250th Anniversary of the United States," said fourth-generation cigar maker Drew Newman. "Cigars have been an important part of our country since its founding. Therefore, it is only fitting that we celebrate America's 250th birthday with cigars handmade in America."

Newman also noted the factory's place in U.S. cigar history: when his great-grandfather founded the family business in 1895, there were 40,000 cigar factories in the United States; today, El Reloj is the last large cigar factory still rolling cigars in the country.
