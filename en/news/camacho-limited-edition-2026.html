Camacho Limited Edition 2026 Lands September 3 in the 11/18 Perfecto Shape

The second 'Boldly Built by Masters' release is an all-new blend in Camacho's 11/18 perfecto at 6 x 56, $17 per cigar and limited to 60,000 cigars, dedicated to the brand's master blenders.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
August 20, 2026
Release
September 3, 2026
Maker
Davidoff

Last year, Davidoff's Camacho brand launched a limited edition series under the theme "Boldly Built by Masters." The sophomore release, the Camacho Limited Edition 2026, was released on September 3 with an all-new blend, a new vitola and new boxes compared with the 2025 edition.

The cigar is offered in Camacho's unique 11/18 perfecto shape, listed at 6 x 56. It uses an Ecuadorian wrapper over a Honduran binder and fillers from the Dominican Republic, Honduras and Nicaragua, and is described as medium-plus. Production is at Diadema Cigars de Honduras S.A.

The theme honors the people who make the cigars: this release is dedicated to the master blenders at Camacho's Master Blending Competence Center, and the inside of the box features an insert with a cut-out in the shape of a chaveta, the tool cigar rollers use to cut the wrapper leaf precisely. The price is $17 per cigar, $255 per box of 15, and production is limited to 60,000 cigars.
