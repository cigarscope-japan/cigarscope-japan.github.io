Foundation Shows No New Cigars at PCA 2026 as the AJ Fernandez Fire Still Casts a Shadow

A year after the fire at the AJ Fernandez factory in Estelí, Foundation Cigar Co. exhibited at PCA 2026 with no new products: finished stock was destroyed, the 10 Year Aniversario shipped at about half its planned run, and new releases are now planned for the end of summer.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
May 1, 2026
Maker
Foundation Cigar Co.

Just weeks before the start of the 2025 PCA Convention & Trade Show, a fire broke out in the packaging room at the main AJ Fernandez factory in Estelí, Nicaragua. A year on, its effects are still visible: at PCA 2026, Foundation Cigar Co. was the most prominent company halfwheel visited that exhibited no new products — an issue directly caused by the fire.

Nicholas Melillo, Foundation's founder, said that a large number of the company's finished cigars still at the factory were damaged or destroyed by the fire, which in turn caused shipping delays for various releases. All of the new items announced last year did end up shipping, just not in the quantities Foundation had planned. The company shipped only about half of the production run of its Foundation 10 Year Aniversario, with boxes expected to continue shipping until September, and the event-only Primer Fábrica 1810 was also affected.

New cigars are in the works, however. Melillo said he has new releases that he is planning to announce by the end of the summer, though he declined to give any more information. The episode shows how a single factory fire can still be shaping a contract brand's release calendar more than a year later.
