Big Smoke Las Vegas Marks 30 Years With Guy Fieri and the Top Cigars of 2025

Cigar Aficionado's Big Smoke Las Vegas celebrates its 30th anniversary on November 6-7 at Horseshoe Las Vegas, headlined by a Guy Fieri interview and seminars built around the magazine's top cigars of 2025.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
September 1, 2026
Maker
Cigar Aficionado

Las Vegas's biggest cigar party turns 30 this year. Cigar Aficionado's Big Smoke Las Vegas 30th Anniversary runs November 6-7 at Horseshoe Las Vegas, combining evening celebrations — where guests collect more than 25 premium cigars — with a daytime seminar programme that usually sells out early and draws more than 500 attendees.

The headliner is celebrity chef Guy Fieri, the magazine's cover star and co-creator, with Erik Espinosa, of the Knuckle Sandwich brands; the pair are also planning to open a cigar factory together in the Dominican Republic. Seminar attendees will smoke along with three of the magazine's top cigars of 2025: the No. 1 Padrón 60th Anniversary Perfecto (97 points, $75), presented by Jorge Padrón; the No. 3 Tatuaje Black Petite Lancero, with Pete Johnson; and the No. 4 Cuesta-Rey Centro Fino Sungrown Pyramid No. 9, introduced by Drew Newman of J.C. Newman.

A 30th anniversary panel brings together Litto Gomez of La Flor Dominicana, José Oliva of Oliva Cigar and Rocky Patel to look back over three decades of the industry, joined by the magazine's editors. The programme also includes an Oliva brunch and a cigars-and-spirits pairing built around Aberfeldy single malt Scotch.
