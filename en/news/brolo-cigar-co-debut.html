Brolo Cigar Co. Debuts November 19 With Three Blends From a Husband-and-Wife Startup

Texas startup Brolo Cigar Co., founded by Joshua and Kaitlyn Stephens, launches with three regular-production robustos — Porchlight, Confessional and Back Booth — at $14.75 to $16.25, debuting in McKinney on November 19.

Published

Maker
Brolo Cigar Co.

A new name joins the boutique end of the market this month. Brolo Cigar Co., a Texas company founded by husband-and-wife team Joshua and Kaitlyn Stephens, will launch its first cigars on November 19 at an event at Viso Cigar Lounge in McKinney, Texas, with shipments to retailers beginning the following day. The couple built an online community around the brand before releasing any cigars, and describe their philosophy as "patina over polish" — character and relationships over perfection and transactions.

The debut is a trio of regular-production blends, all in a single 5 x 50 robusto size packed in 20-count boxes. Porchlight pairs an Ecuadorian corojo wrapper with a Nicaraguan binder and Nicaraguan and Dominican fillers, at $14.75. Confessional, also $14.75, is a habano-forward blend with an Ecuadorian habano rosado wrapper and Nicaraguan binder and fillers. Back Booth, the richest of the three at $16.25, uses a Mexican San Andrés maduro wrapper over Nicaraguan and Dominican fillers.

"There's something special about the people you meet around cigars," said co-founder Joshua Stephens. "For an hour or two, none of that really matters. You're just sitting together, sharing something good. That's the spirit we wanted to capture with Brolo." The company has not disclosed which factory makes its cigars.
