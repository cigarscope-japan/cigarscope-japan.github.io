STG — European Handmade Cigar Sales Down About 20% as Strong U.S. Demand Strains Supply

Scandinavian Tobacco Group's Q3 2026 results show European handmade cigar sales down 20.5% year on year, from DKK 92.9 million to DKK 73.9 million in the first nine months. STG blames a supply shortage, not weak European demand: stronger-than-expected U.S. demand left too little for Europe. France and Spain are hit hardest; full recovery is expected early next year.

Published
November 18, 2026
Announced
November 17, 2026
Maker
Scandinavian Tobacco Group

According to Cigars-connect (L'Amateur de Cigare), Scandinavian Tobacco Group's Q3 2026 results show European handmade cigar sales in its European Brands division falling 20.5% — from DKK 92.9 million (about €12.43 million) in January–September 2025 to DKK 73.9 million (about €9.89 million). STG says the cause is not weak European demand but a supply shortage: U.S. demand was stronger than expected, leaving too little supply for Europe. France and Spain are hit hardest. Supply is gradually recovering, but a full recovery is not expected until early next year. In the United States, handmade cigar consumption continues to decline, and STG expects the 2026 decline to be slightly smaller than the 4% it originally forecast. Group-wide, handmade cigars grew 4% organically in January–September, but slowed to 1% in the third quarter. Our November 16 edition covered the same results from the "handmade cigars strong, full-year outlook raised" angle; this is the other side of the story — the European supply shortfall.

STG Raises Its Full-Year Outlook as Handmade Cigars Lead a Strong Third Quarter

Scandinavian Tobacco Group (STG) reported its January–September 2026 results and raised its full-year outlook. Third-quarter revenue was DKK 2,434 million, up 3 percent year on year, with EBITDA before special items up 18 percent and net profit up 30 percent. Handmade cigars kept growing, with organic revenue up 4 percent, and the full-year EBIT margin guidance was raised to 14.0–15.5 percent.
