Habanos Festival Humidors Head to a Charity Auction in Macau on November 13

Four DeART humidors built for the cancelled 26th Habanos Festival, holding 900 Cuban cigars in total, will be auctioned for charity in Macau on November 13, with no reserves, no buyer's premiums and all proceeds going to Cuba's public healthcare system.

Published
November 11, 2026
Announced
November 10, 2026
Maker
The Pacific Cigar Co.

Four festival humidors made by Italy's DeART for the 26th Habanos Festival — postponed in February 2026 and subsequently cancelled, which cost them their traditional gala auction in Havana — will finally go under the hammer in Macau on Friday, November 13, London Cigar Smoker reported. The Pacific Cigar Company hosts the charity sale, titled "The Tradition Endures: The Habano Festival 2026 Humidor Auction," alongside a gala dinner, with online bidding through Madison Auction.

The four humidors hold a combined 900 Cuban cigars: the Diplomáticos 60 Aniversario humidor with 160 cigars, the Rafael González 90 Aniversario with 190, the Cuaba 30 Aniversario with 300, and a fourth humidor created specifically for the Macau event, holding 250 Saint Luis Rey cigars including 50 of the new Palatinos vitola.

Bidding opens at 9:15 p.m. Hong Kong time on November 13. There are no reserve prices and no buyer's premiums, and, as with the traditional festival auction, all proceeds go to Cuba's public healthcare system. The humidors themselves had a long journey: shipped to Cuba, returned to DeART in Italy, and then sent on to Macau.
