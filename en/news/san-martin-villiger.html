Villiger's San Martín: A New Handmade Premium Brand Named for South America's Liberator

Villiger's 2026 premium launch is handmade at Tabacalera ABAM in the Dominican Republic, with a deliberately undisclosed all-Caribbean blend, in robusto, piramides and churchill formats in boxes of 20.

Published
November 9, 2026
Announced
October 19, 2026
Release
April 30, 2026
Maker
Villiger
Share Post on X Share on LINE Facebook

Villiger is best known for machine-made cigars, but the Swiss group's premium ambitions have a new flagship. San Martín, launched in 2026, is a handmade brand named for José de San Martín (1778-1850), the general who helped liberate Argentina, Chile and Peru. Its world premiere was held on April 30, 2026, at a "San Martín Caribbean Night" in Gronau, Germany.

The cigars are hand-rolled at Tabacalera ABAM in the Dominican Republic, under the responsibility of Matías Maragoto, who also oversees the Villiger 1888 line. The blend is deliberately undisclosed — secrecy is part of the concept — beyond the fact that the tobaccos are 100 percent Caribbean. Retailer descriptions put the profile in creamy, earthy territory, with cedar, leather, coffee and roasted nuts, and a subtle citrus lift noted by Geneva's House of Grauer.

The core formats are a Robusto (50 x 124 mm), a Pirámides (52 x 156 mm) and a Churchill (48 x 178 mm), joined by a Robusto Tubo, all in boxes of 20. German retail prices run from €15.50 for the Robusto to €17.50. For a house founded in 1888 and built on machine-made volume, San Martín is positioned as the new pillar of its handmade premium side.
