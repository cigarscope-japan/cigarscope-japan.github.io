Editorial Policy

This page states the rules the CigarScope Japan desk works by. They apply to the English edition in full.

Original summaries, never republication

English stories are written by our desk from the original sources — manufacturers' announcements and established cigar media — and must stand on their own. We do not republish machine translations, and we do not present another outlet's reporting as our own: the source of every story is named and linked.

Verification

Before publication, every number, name, date and product detail in a story is checked against its source in a claim-by-claim mapping table kept in our audit files. Three dates are always kept distinct: the date a story was announced, the date a product is released, and the date we publish. A vague release window is never sharpened into a precise date.

Facts, reports and judgement

What a source states as fact, what is merely reported, and what is our editorial judgement are labelled as such. Rumour and speculation are graded and flagged, never dressed as fact. Where a story rests on secondary reporting, it says so; where the subject of a report has answered or denied a claim, that position is carried in the story.

Independence

We accept no advertising and no payment for coverage. One outlet is excluded from our sourcing altogether as an editorial-independence measure to protect the accuracy of our reporting; themes it has covered are published only when independently confirmed through other sources. Exclusions are recorded in our audit files, separately from our source grading.

Corrections

Errors are corrected openly: a dated note on the article itself, an entry in the article's history, and a record on the Corrections page. Factual content is never silently rewritten.
