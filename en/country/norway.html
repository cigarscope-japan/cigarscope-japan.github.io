Norway's Augusto Cigars Overturns a Ruinous Registration Fee — a Bill Worth a Third of Its Revenue Is Frozen

When Norway introduced the EU Tobacco Products Directive registration system in March 2026, the health authorities suddenly announced a fee of EUR 800 per SKU plus EUR 300 a year. For Augusto Cigars alone the first-year bill came to EUR 345,000 — about a third of its EUR 1.2 million revenue — and the four Norwegian companies faced more than EUR 2 million combined. The company refused to register and appealed directly to the Ministry of Health, which admitted the error and froze collection; after legislation expected in early 2027, its burden should fall to about EUR 1,800.
