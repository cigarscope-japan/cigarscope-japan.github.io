Casa Carrillo's veterans tribute has come back with almost everything changed. The Perez-Carrillo Battleborn, which debuted last year, returns for 2026 with a new blend, a new size and new colours: the Heroes is a 6 x 52 toro with a Cameroon wrapper over Dominican and Nicaraguan fillers, priced at $21.

The format has changed in the details, too. Because the cigars are thinner, boxes now hold 21 cigars instead of 20 — $441 per box — and each box includes a custom dog tag. Production is limited to 42,000 cigars. Launch events were held on Oct. 22 at Old Virginia Tobacco Co.'s Kingstowne store and on Oct. 23 at its Woodbridge store, and Casa Carrillo said the cigars would ship nationwide before Veterans Day, Nov. 11.

Made to honour veterans

The series was inspired by Lionel H. McKoy, a U.S. Navy veteran who served for two decades and joined Casa Carrillo in 2017 as its shipping manager. The release raises money for veterans' charities, though the company has not specified which charities will receive donations. Last year's first release, Warriors, was a larger 6 x 56 toro gordo with a Connecticut broadleaf wrapper and green camouflage colours; the 2026 edition is primarily orange. The cigars are made at the Casa Carrillo factory in the Dominican Republic.
