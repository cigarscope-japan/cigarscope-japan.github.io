JRE Tobacco showed the cigar at the 2026 PCA Convention & Trade Show; now it has a shipping window. The Aladino 85 Aniversario Reserva Elegante is a 7 x 38 lancero and the third vitola in the 85 Aniversario Reserva line, which debuted last year as a belated 85th birthday cigar for company founder Julio R. Eiroa.

A showcase for pinareño

For this blend, Eiroa wanted to show off the pinareño seed. Pinareño was brought from Cuba to Central America at around the same time as corojo, but the lighter seed fell out of favour because corojo offered better disease resistance and yield. It has returned recently in releases from both JRE Tobacco, which Eiroa owns with his son Justo, and CLE Cigar Co., owned by his son Christian. Wrapper, binder and filler are all Honduran pinareño.

The Elegante is regular production, but distribution is deliberately narrow: it will only be offered to stores that carry both the 85 Aniversario Reserva Robusto and Toro, and those stores are limited to two boxes per month. Pricing is $22 per cigar and $440 for a box of 20, with shipping scheduled during the week of Nov. 9.
