Drew Estate has put ten different blends into a single shape. The Feral Flying Pig Collection is a ten-cigar sampler in the 5 3/8 x 60 Feral Flying Pig vitola, and only two of the cigars in the box have been sold in that size before: the original Liga Privada Único Serie Feral Flying Pig and the Liga Privada H99 Feral Flying Pig. The other eight are first-time releases in the vitola.

The line-up spans the company's portfolio: Herrera Estelí Brazil and Herrera Estelí Habano, Nica Rustica Shade and Nica Rustica Adobe, Liga Privada Único Serie, T52 and H99 Connecticut Corojo, and Undercrown Shade, El Tigre Dominicano and 10. Each sampler has an MSRP of $170, and the collection was released on Oct. 19. It is currently awaiting approval for sale in California.

A box-pressed first, sold separately

Alongside the sampler, the Undercrown El Tigre Dominicano Flying Pig uses the shorter 3 15/16 x 60 Flying Pig vitola — but box-pressed, which appears to be the first time a box-pressed Flying Pig has been offered for sale. It is priced at $15.70 per cigar and $188.40 for a box of 12.

Two of the collection's blends, Herrera Estelí Brazil and Liga Privada H99, are also offered as box-pressed Feral Flying Pigs in a separate three-count sampler introduced last month. Both new items are limited releases, though Drew Estate has not said how limited either of them is.
