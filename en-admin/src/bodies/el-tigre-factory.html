Drew Estate's newest Undercrown line is also the vehicle for a production shift. The Undercrown El Tigre Dominicano, introduced earlier this year, is currently made at La Gran Fábrica Drew Estate in Estelí, Nicaragua. The company has said it plans to move production to its upcoming Dominican factory, which it expects to open next year.

The blend points in the same direction: an Ecuadorian Sumatra-seed wrapper over a Connecticut corojo H99 hybrid binder, with Dominican criollo 98 and HVA fillers. A Dominican-made line, built around Dominican filler, would give Drew Estate a second production base alongside its Nicaraguan heartland.

The move was reported alongside the line's new Flying Pig release (see the related story). No opening date beyond “next year” has been given for the Dominican factory, and the company has not said which other lines, if any, would follow El Tigre there.
