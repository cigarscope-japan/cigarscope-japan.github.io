The CigarScope Japan desk reads every message. The reliable way to reach us is the issue tracker of our public site repository on GitHub, which we use for corrections, tips and general contact.

Corrections

If you spot an error in a story, please open an issue naming the article and the point in question. Verified errors are corrected openly under our Editorial Policy and recorded on the Corrections page.

News tips

Announcements, release information and corrections from makers, distributors and retailers are welcome. Please include the primary source — an official announcement or document — wherever possible; unconfirmed tips are treated as leads, not as facts.

What we cannot do

CigarScope Japan sells nothing and takes no orders, reservations or payments. For purchases, please contact the retailer concerned.
