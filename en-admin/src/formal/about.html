CigarScope Japan is an independent editorial desk in Japan that covers the world premium cigar industry — not one country, not one brand. Our Japanese edition has published since 2026; this English edition presents original English summaries prepared by our desk from the sources we cite.

What we cover

New releases and limited editions, the companies and families behind them, factories and farms, markets and distribution, and the culture that surrounds the premium cigar — from Cuba, Nicaragua and the Dominican Republic to Honduras, the United States and emerging regions beyond. Our motto is the one in the masthead: beyond Havana, beyond borders.

How we work

Every story names its source and links to the original. Facts, reported claims and editorial judgement are kept distinct, and unconfirmed information is labelled as such — or left out. We accept no advertising, sell nothing, and take no payment for coverage. Corrections are published openly on our Corrections page.

Editions

The Japanese edition is our home edition and carries our long-form features. English feature articles are prepared separately, on their own approval, rather than translated mechanically.
