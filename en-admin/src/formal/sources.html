CigarScope Japan grades its sources in five tiers and judges each article against the tier of the source it rests on — never by a famous name alone.

The tiers

  • T1 — Primary. The announcing body's own official channel: a maker's announcement, an official statement.
  • T2 — Specialist editorial media. Cigar-specialist outlets that cite and link primary sources, maintain a corrections record, and name their reporters and editorial responsibility — for example Halfwheel and Cigar Aficionado.
  • T3 — Industry and general press. Editorial outlets that are not cigar-specialist, such as Tobacco Reporter.
  • T4 — Personal, community and retailer pages. Used for corroboration and for observing prices and stock only, never as the sole basis of a story.
  • T5 — Untraceable. Never used as a basis for publication.

How a story's source is shown

Every article carries a Source box naming the outlet and linking to the original text, so readers can check our summary against it directly. Where a story rests on secondary reporting rather than a primary announcement, the article's framing says so.

Exclusions

One outlet is excluded from our sourcing as an editorial-independence measure (see the Editorial Policy). Excluded outlets are recorded separately from this grading, and no story is ever based on them.
