CigarScope Japan is a static website. We run no accounts, no comment system, no advertising trackers and no analytics of our own.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any website, the hosting provider receives the ordinary requests your browser makes to load pages, images and files, and may log them under its own policies. That is the only server-side data flow involved in reading this site.

Search

Search on this site runs entirely in your browser. Your search terms are never sent to us, to the hosting provider as a query, or to any third-party search service: the search index file is downloaded as-is, matching happens on your device, and the hand-off between pages uses the part of the URL after the “#” sign, which browsers do not transmit to servers.

Two things are worth knowing. Your search terms can remain in your own browser history. And if you share a link that carries a search term after the “#” sign, the person you share it with will see the term. Both are under your control, on your device.

External links

Stories link to their original sources and to other sites. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you follow a link.
